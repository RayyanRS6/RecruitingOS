# 12. API contracts

All routes are under `/api/v1`, JSON in and out, validated with the Zod schemas in `packages/contracts/src/api/*`. Errors are `{error: {code, message, details?}}` with HTTP status. Authentication is a session cookie; platform-admin routes additionally require `platform_role`. Tenancy is derived from the session's active membership (`X-Company-Id` selects among memberships). Every mutating route emits the events listed in doc 04. Candidate-facing routes are token-scoped and live under `/c/*` (pages) and `/api/c/*` (JSON).

The schemas are the source of truth; this document is the map.

## Intake and roles
The job builder (`apps/api/src/routes/intake.ts`, plan Wave C). Every intake route needs `role.create` and `compensation.read` (a draft carries pay). The draft rule: a draft is opened and changed by the person who started it, by any owner or admin, and by an ITS recruiter whose accepted request is about that draft; anyone else gets 403 `not_your_draft`, and the person who started a draft that was taken over gets 403 `taken_over`. An ITS recruiter under a grant for one role or one candidate never starts or opens a draft (403 `staff_scope`). A typed message over the limit is 400 `message_too_long`, with the limit in the message.
