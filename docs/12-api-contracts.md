# 12. API contracts

All routes are under `/api/v1`, JSON in and out, validated with the Zod schemas in `packages/contracts/src/api/*`. Errors are `{error: {code, message, details?}}` with HTTP status. Authentication is a session cookie; platform-admin routes additionally require `platform_role`. Tenancy is derived from the session's active membership (`X-Company-Id` selects among memberships). Every mutating route emits the events listed in doc 04. Candidate-facing routes are token-scoped and live under `/c/*` (pages) and `/api/c/*` (JSON).

The schemas are the source of truth; this document is the map.

## Intake and roles
The job builder (`apps/api/src/routes/intake.ts`, plan Wave C). Every intake route needs `role.create` and `compensation.read` (a draft carries pay). The draft rule: a draft is opened and changed by the person who started it, by any owner or admin, and by an ITS recruiter whose accepted request is about that draft; anyone else gets 403 `not_your_draft`, and the person who started a draft that was taken over gets 403 `taken_over`. An ITS recruiter under a grant for one role or one candidate never starts or opens a draft (403 `staff_scope`). A typed message over the limit is 400 `message_too_long`, with the limit in the message.

## Candidates and applications
**Someone locked (the plans of 5 November 2026, Rule 3).** `applications.locked_at` says who is past the plan's candidates per job, and the server is the wall. A guard mounted in `apps/api/src/app.ts` at the `/api/v1/applications/:id` and `/api/v1/interviews/:id` prefixes answers 402 `candidate_locked` before any handler, on every route in this section and "Interviews and scheduling" that takes such an id, so no signed link is minted and no `candidate.viewed` is written. An id not written the way the database writes it (lower case, hyphens in their places) answers 404 there too, for everyone signed in, because Postgres would read the other forms and find the row. Only a grant naming exactly that application (a candidate who asked for a person) and break glass go through; someone on the team and an ITS recruiter under a grant for a role or the company are refused alike. Those two still start nothing: `invite_screen`, `ask_in_screen`, `interview`, `advance_to_interview` and `POST .../interviews` answer 409 `not_while_locked`, as does `GET .../interview-draft`, `POST .../messages/suggest` and approving a waiting invitation or recommendation, and they read them as having no AI interview coming (`aiInterview` null). A message or decision message they send goes without the company's approval rule, because nobody there may read it, with a `message.approval_skipped` audit line. Every other reader filters in the query, before anything is ordered or read. A locked id in a body or a query (compare, batch, decision messages, new times, the questions) answers as an id not in the role; an approval or a recruiter request about someone locked answers 402. `GET /events` and `/roles/:id/timeline` hold back entries about them, and the pool, Home and the people pages never name them.
