# 04. Screen and state inventory (release 1)

Status column: **built** (exists and stays), **changed** (exists, modified in release 1), **new**. Every screen must implement the standard states: loading (skeletons, never spinners in content), empty (one sentence, one action), error (what happened, what to do, retry), forbidden (plain explanation), and success (toast or inline confirmation). Owner mode and candidate pages are designed at 375 px first; WCAG 2.2 AA applies everywhere.

Accessibility (18 Sep): every screen in the browser journey passes axe WCAG 2.1 A/AA; the design tokens were darkened where text fell below 4.5:1, every page has a `main` landmark, loading states use `role="status"`, action columns have hidden headers; candidate pages remain usable without JavaScript and by keyboard alone.

Global states: signed out → /login; signed in without a company → /setup/company (staff → queue); a fake AI provider shows a caution banner (development only; production refuses to boot); a disconnected calendar or a pending email domain shows a persistent Setup badge; the pilot plan near its limits shows a Today line.

## 4. Notification kinds (in-app)

Existing: application.needs_review, application.ready, screen.invited, screen.completed, screen.expired, screen.human_requested, candidate.recommended, candidate.withdrawn, entitlement.text_screens, stuck.*. New: interview.slots_offered, interview.scheduled, interview.rescheduled, interview.cancelled, interview.no_slot_chosen, interview.completed_decide, calendar.needs_attention, escalation.resolved, data_request.received (owner/admin/hr), intake.taken_over (to the person who started a draft an owner or admin took over; Wave C). The Cancelled column (founder decisions 1A to 4A, 10 Oct 2026): `interview.cancelled` from a candidate's own cancel says their choice and whether new times go automatically (and on which day), or why not; `interview.no_show` ("{name} did not come to the interview", replacing "How did it go?" for a candidate no-show); `interview.new_times_sent` ("New times went to {name}", when the automatic set's email is on its way); `interview.new_times_not_sent` ("New times for {name} were not sent", with the reason); `interview.new_times` to the interviewers ("New times are being set up for {name}"). All link to the candidate.

Built 30 Sep 2026 (audit F4, F14): `interview.assigned` goes to each interviewer the moment a round is approved ("You are interviewing {name}", link to the interview; their participant row is `invited` until the booking makes it `confirmed`); what the draft called `interview.completed_decide` is two kinds, `interview.outcome` to the participants ("Did the interview with {name} take place?", link to the interview) and `interview.decision` to the hiring team ("How did it go with {name}?"). Every notification and Today card about one candidate links to `/roles/{roleId}/talent?open={applicationId}`, which opens that candidate's sheet directly; a link about a list stays `/roles/{roleId}/talent`.

## 5. Accessibility and content rules

Keyboard operability for every action; visible focus; labels on every input; error text tied to fields; live region for conversation and chat threads; color never the sole carrier of meaning (category chips carry text); reduced-motion respected; candidate pages work without JavaScript. Copy follows `docs/07`: no banned words; every automated candidate message carries the disclosure; nothing says "done" for an action that needs approval.
