# Onboarding checklist

**ITS AI Recruiting Department, Founding Partner Program. Release 1.**

Draft of 30 September 2026 (checked again against the product the same day; the company check and the Getting started card added 8 October 2026), for review by ITS before it is given to a customer. This is the order of events from signing to your first candidate, who does each step, and what you need to have ready.

## Who does what

- **ITS**: your ITS contact and the ITS recruiters.
- **Owner**: the person at your company who signs the agreement and owns the workspace. Often the business owner or office manager.
- **Hiring manager**: the person who runs the hire, describes the role and decides on candidates. This can be the same person as the owner.
- **Interviewers**: the people who will meet candidates.

## What to have ready before you start

- **Your sign-in accounts.** A Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 work account for each person who will use the product, with two-step verification switched on. A Microsoft 365 company is the simplest fit.
- **Your job-board accounts.** An employer account on LinkedIn (a company page) and Indeed that your company owns or is authorized to use, and a ZipRecruiter account only if you have your own paid ZipRecruiter plan (say so in **Setup**, **Job boards**; ZipRecruiter is offered only then). ITS posts under these accounts, unless you choose in **Setup** that your team posts them. Decide who at your company administers each one.
- **Your company basics.** Company name, what kind of business, roughly how many employees, city and state, website.
- **A short description of your company** for the job page: two or three sentences a candidate would want to read. A one-line introduction for your careers page and a short benefits summary help too.
- **Your logo**, as an image file (PNG, JPEG or WebP, under 2 MB), if you want it on your careers page, your job pages and in search results. You upload it in **Setup**; we keep a copy and show it from our own address.
- **A few sentences about your company** for your job pages. The assistant drafts them from what you told us; you change what you like and approve them in **Setup**. Nothing shows until you approve it.
- **Who candidates can write to before they apply**, for help or a change to how they apply because of a disability. You set it in **Setup**, under **How candidates see you**. Without one, the job pages show the Identified Talent Solutions inbox that answers candidates for you.
- **The role.** The job title, where the job is, whether it is on site, the schedule, the pay range and the most you would pay, why you are hiring, the target start date, the must-haves (up to six), and what you would like but do not require.
- **Who interviews and who decides.** Names and work email addresses.
- **Who handles privacy questions on your side**, if a candidate writes to you directly.

## What ITS sets up before your first candidate

You do not need to do anything for these. They are listed so you know what is happening behind the scenes and can ask about them.

- **The company check.** When you create your workspace, ITS makes a one-time check that your company is real, usually within a business day (often on the kick-off call). Until ITS confirms it, you can draft and approve roles, but your careers page, job pages and Google listing stay offline, and any email to a candidate waits; nothing is lost, and the waiting emails go out on their own the moment ITS confirms. **Today** says so while you wait, with how many emails are waiting, and you are told when it is done.
- **The job-page address.** Your careers page and job pages move to our job address, jobs.identifiedtalent.com. Until then they are served from the main product address. Either way they work; the job address is what goes on the boards.
- **The malware scanner.** Every resume is scanned before anyone on your team can open it. ITS starts the scanning service before any real candidate applies. Until it runs, no real candidate information is accepted.
- **Incoming mail.** ITS connects the mail address candidates reply to. Once it is connected, a candidate's reply to any email appears on their page and your team is told, and applications forwarded by email arrive on their own. Until then, replies reach an ITS mailbox that a person reads, and the boards use their application links only.

## The steps

| # | Step | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign the agreement | Owner, ITS | The agreement with its exhibits (the feature sheet, the security overview, the support statement) is signed. You also receive this checklist and the two guides. ITS issues the first invoice under the agreement. No payment processor is involved; you pay the invoice the way the agreement says |
| 2 | Kick-off call | Owner, hiring manager, ITS | About forty-five minutes. ITS confirms your ITS contact, the support and privacy addresses, the weekly meeting time, and which of your people get which role. ITS walks through the feature sheet so nobody expects something the product does not do |
| 3 | Sign in and create the workspace | Owner | Go to the sign-in page and press **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**. Fill in **Tell us about your company** and press **Create my hiring workspace**. The person who creates the workspace is its owner. **Today** then shows a **Getting started** card with the setup steps below (company details, team, interview hours, the first role, job-board access, the AI rules), only the ones you can do, each with a line on why it matters, ticked as you finish them. Each person on your team sees their own steps and can hide them with **Hide these steps**; the card steps aside on its own once there are decisions waiting for you. Then, in **Setup**, press **Edit** on the **Company** card to add your website, careers-page introduction and benefits summary, and press **Save changes**. Under **How candidates see you**, upload your logo, approve a few sentences about the company (the assistant can draft them) and, if you like, set who candidates can write to before they apply |
| 4 | Invite the team | Owner | In **Setup**, under **Team**, enter each person's work email under **Invite by email**, their name if you like, choose their **Role** (Admin, Hiring manager, Interviewer or HR) and press **Invite**. Each person gets one email. The link works for seven days. People not yet in show under **Invited, not yet in**, with **Send again** and **Withdraw** |
| 5 | Everyone signs in once | Everyone invited | Open the invitation email, follow the link and sign in with Google or Microsoft using the same work address. Opening the link alone does not sign anyone in. Check that the workspace name shows at the top left |
| 6 | Interviewers set their availability | Interviewers, hiring manager | In **Setup**, under **My availability for interviews**, each interviewer sets their time zone, the days and hours they can interview, the buffer between interviews and the most interviews per day, then presses **Save availability**. If ITS has enabled Google Calendar for your workspace, they can press **Connect Google Calendar** instead. Until someone has availability, interview times cannot be offered |
| 7 | Review what the assistant may do on its own | Owner | In **Setup**, under **What the AI may do on its own**, read the list. The defaults are the recommended settings. The decisions that always stay with a person are marked "always a person" and cannot be changed. One setting to decide now: whether messages your team writes to candidates go out at once (the default) or wait for an owner or admin to read them. If you signed in more than 30 minutes ago, the product asks you to **Confirm with my sign-in** before it saves a change. When you are done, press **I've reviewed them** on the Getting started card |
| 8 | Give ITS access to your job-board accounts | Owner, ITS | Add the ITS recruiter named by your ITS contact as a user on your LinkedIn company page and your Indeed employer account (and your ZipRecruiter account, if you have your own paid plan), or agree another way with ITS. If your team will post on the boards itself, choose **Our team posts them** under **Job boards** in **Setup** instead and skip this step. Do not send passwords by email. ITS confirms it can sign in to each, and the step on the Getting started card ticks |
| 9 | Draft the first role in the job builder | Hiring manager | On **Today**, type into **Who are you hiring?** a sentence or two about the role, or paste a job ad you already have (or press **Start a hire** in the menu or on the Getting started card). The job builder opens: answer the assistant's question cards, set the pay on the **Pay** card, and press **Write the draft**. Check the draft beside the conversation, ask for any change, then press **Ask for approval**. The hiring manager guide walks through it. To draft the first role with a person instead, press **Draft it with an ITS recruiter** on the card: the request form opens filled in and shows, before you send it, what the recruiter will see and for how long, when they will reply, and that it uses one of your plan's recruiter requests |
| 10 | Review the role packet | Hiring manager, owner, ITS | On the role page, read the tabs Overview, Requirements, How we'll evaluate, Interview plan and Posting (Activity, History and Report fill in as the role runs). Check the pay range and the ceiling, the work model, the must-haves (each says why it matters and where it is checked), the eligibility questions, the screening questions and the interview plan. Keep the screening questions to five or six: candidates answer them in writing straight after the form, in about ten minutes. Under **What matters to you**, check the deal-breakers and who makes the final decision. Press **Edit first** to change anything. ITS reviews the packet with you in the kick-off week if you want a second pair of eyes |
| 11 | Approve the role | Owner or hiring manager | Press **Preview the job page** to see it exactly as candidates will (a new tab; never public), then **Approve and publish**. The card also says which job boards it goes to (change the ticks there), what the law where the job is still asks of the ad, and what starts on its own once it is live. The person who ran the intake cannot approve their own role unless they are the company's only owner, so where you have two people the other one approves; if the packet names a decision maker on your team, the approval is theirs (an owner can also decide). To ask for changes instead, press **Send back with a note**: the role becomes a draft again with the note on its page, and **Request approval again** sends it back once it is edited. Approval opens the role: the careers page and job page go live, the Google job data is on the page, and a short screen shows where the job is going, each place ticking only once it was checked, with **Share it now**. If ITS has not confirmed your company yet, the approval card says so and the role is approved and waits: the role page and its Posting tab say why the job page shows "not found" meanwhile, and it goes public by itself the moment ITS confirms |
| 12 | ITS posts to the boards | ITS | ITS gets a task for each board ticked for the role and posts the job there under your accounts, using the text, the steps and each board's own application link; the **Posting** tab shows who at ITS has each board. If ITS cannot post one, it says why on the **Posting** tab and **Today**, and you ask ITS again or post it yourself. (If your team posts itself, your team does this step from the **Posting** tab.) ITS records each posting's public address; the product opens the page and checks it shows your job; the posting is then marked **Live and verified**. Until then it shows **Ready to post** or **Posted**. Your careers page goes through the same check on its own |
| 13 | First applications arrive | Nobody has to do anything | Each candidate gets a confirmation and a status page. On a role where ITS switched on the conversation in the same visit (pilot roles first), everyone whose eligibility answers pass goes straight from the form to a short written conversation with your AI assistant (five or six questions, about ten minutes; they can do it later or ask for a person instead) and shows under **Still applying** until they send it; you see their answers only once they do. The resume is checked and scanned and the assistant reads it beside the conversation; the reading, and the conversation's scorecard once the answers are sent, appear in **Talent**. On other roles ("form now, conversation later"), or when your rule for screen invitations is "Ask me first", candidates send the form only and the conversation follows by email |
| 14 | First decisions | Hiring manager | Open **Today** each morning. Review candidates under **Waiting on you** in **Talent**. Invite to a screen, hold, interview, or decline with a reason. Press **Bring in a recruiter** whenever you want a second opinion |

## Week one

- **Every business day**, your ITS contact checks your workspace: applications arriving, messages delivered, postings live, anything waiting on you for more than a day. You get a short note if something needs attention.
- **The hiring manager** opens Today each morning and clears what needs them. Ten minutes is usually enough.
- **The owner** checks Setup once to confirm the team and the interviewers' availability are right.
- **By the end of the week** you should have: the job live on your careers page and on the three boards, applications with their source showing, the first screening conversations completed, and the first interview approved and scheduled. The role's **Report** tab shows where everyone is.
- **The first weekly meeting** at the end of the week: what worked, what confused anyone, what to change in the packet, and the first written summary from ITS.

## After week one

- The weekly meeting and the written summary continue for the program.
- When you have hired, record the outcome on the candidate and close the role. The product lists the boards where the posting is still up; ITS takes them down and ticks each one off on the **Posting** tab.
- When someone leaves your company, remove them in **Setup**, under **Team**, the same day.
- Candidates who were still active stay in your talent pool for the next role.
