# Support expectations

**ITS AI Recruiting Department, Founding Partner Program. Release 1.**

Draft of 30 September 2026, for review by ITS before it is given to a customer. This statement is attached to your agreement. It says when we are available, how to reach us, how quickly we respond, what support covers, and how we tell you about problems.

## 1. Hours

- ITS support hours are **Monday to Friday, 9 to 5 Pacific time**.
- We are closed on US federal holidays.
- ITS may publish a closure or a temporary change of hours with an effective date. When a request is sent, its deadline is worked out from the calendar in force at that moment. A deadline already set does not move unless ITS recalculates it with a reason, and you see that change on the request.

## 2. How to reach us

**Your team**

- **Bring in a recruiter**, inside the product. The button is on Today, on every role and on every candidate. Pick what you need (Review these candidates, Help me decide, Take this role over), add a note if you like, mark it Normal or Urgent, and press **Send to a recruiter**. You see the expected response time straight away, and the request appears on Today until it is answered.
- **support@identifiedtalent.com** for anything else: a question about the product, a change to your team or company details, something that looks wrong, or a problem that is blocking you. If it is blocking your hiring, say so in the subject line.
- Your ITS contact for the program is the person named in your agreement. In the first week, that person checks your workspace every business day.

**Candidates**

- Reply **HUMAN** to any email from the product.
- Press **Talk to a person instead** during a screening conversation.
- Use the form on their status page to ask for a copy of their information, a correction, deletion, or a review by a person.
- Email **privacy@identifiedtalent.com** for privacy requests.

## 3. Response times

Response times count **business hours only**, on the ITS calendar above. A business day is eight business hours. The time we promise is the time by which an ITS recruiter has picked up the request and responded, not necessarily the time by which the matter is fully resolved.

| Request | Who starts it | Response within |
|---|---|---|
| Help me decide (advice on a hire) | Your team, in the product | 8 business hours (one business day) |
| Review these candidates | Your team, in the product | 8 business hours |
| Take this role over | Your team, in the product | 8 business hours |
| A held application that nobody on your team decided on for three days | The product, on its own | 8 business hours |
| A role that has stalled despite reminders | The product, on its own | 8 business hours |
| A candidate asks for a person, or the assistant could not continue with a candidate | The candidate, or the product | 4 business hours |
| Something in the product is blocking your hiring | Your team, by email with "blocking" in the subject | 2 business hours |
| A privacy request from a candidate | The candidate | Acknowledged within one business day; completed within the tracked deadline (proposed 45 days) |
| Any other question by email | Your team, by email | Next business day |

Examples of how business hours count:

- A request sent at 10:00 Pacific on a Tuesday with an eight-hour response time is due by 10:00 on Wednesday.
- A request sent at 4:00 pm Pacific on a Friday with a four-hour response time is due by noon on Monday.
- A request sent on a Saturday with a two-hour response time is due by 11:00 on Monday.

**What "Urgent" does.** Marking a request Urgent moves it to the top of the recruiter queue. It does not shorten the promised response time.

**How you see the answer.** When the recruiter responds, the answer appears on Today under **What the assistant did** as "(name) (ITS recruiter) responded", and on the candidate's timeline. If the recruiter needs to act inside your workspace, their access is limited to that request and every page they open is recorded for you.

## 4. What support covers

- Using the product: sign-in, inviting your team, Setup, roles, Talent, Interviews.
- The manual posting of your approved job to LinkedIn, Indeed and ZipRecruiter under your accounts, checking each posting, and taking postings down when a role closes.
- Recruiting help through Bring in a recruiter: reviewing candidates, advice on a decision, running a role for you.
- Candidates who ask for a person, cannot be reached, or have a problem with a screening or scheduling link.
- Privacy requests from candidates.
- Changes ITS has to make for you until the screen exists: removing a team member, changing someone's role, editing company details, correcting a recorded outcome.
- Problems with the product itself, and incidents.

## 5. What support does not cover

- **Hiring decisions.** ITS recruiters advise; your team decides. We never decline, rank or hire on your behalf.
- **Legal advice.** We do not advise on employment law, pay transparency, discrimination law or your obligations as an employer. The product supports compliance (pay ranges where required, lawful eligibility questions), but it is not a substitute for your own counsel.
- **Offers, background checks, references, onboarding and payroll.** These happen outside the product; you record the outcome.
- **Paid promotion on job boards.** Sponsorship is bought by you, from the board, on your own account.
- **Your accounts elsewhere.** Your Google or Microsoft accounts, your job-board accounts, your calendar, your email and your devices are yours to run and secure.
- **Features outside release 1.** If you ask for something the product does not do, we log it for the founder's decision and tell you. We do not build it during the pilot.
- **Support on weekends and holidays**, except for a suspected data exposure (section 6).

## 6. How incidents and outages are communicated

| What happened | First response | What you hear from us |
|---|---|---|
| We suspect your data or a candidate's data was exposed | Within one hour, any day | We tell the owner and admins within one business day of confirming it: what was exposed, whose, over what period, what we did, and what you should do. Counsel is engaged before any notice to candidates or a regulator |
| The product is unavailable, or candidates cannot be reached, for more than thirty minutes | Within two business hours | An email to the owner and admins when we know, updates until it is fixed, and a note when it is resolved |
| A feature is degraded but there is a way around it | Next business day | An email with the workaround and the expected fix |

After a serious incident you receive a written note within five business days: what happened, the timeline, the cause and what we changed.

If a message to a candidate fails to arrive, the product tells you on the candidate's page and on Today, and a person at ITS follows up if the candidate cannot be reached.

## 7. The weekly rhythm

- A weekly meeting with your ITS contact during the program.
- A written weekly summary from ITS: applicants by source, where candidates stand, interviews scheduled and completed, decisions waiting on you, requests you sent us and how quickly we answered, candidates we could not reach, and open privacy requests. This continues until the role report exists in the product.

## 8. Changes to this statement

ITS may change hours, holidays or the calendar with an effective date, published in advance. Response times in section 3 change only with notice to you in writing.
