# Recruiting OS and Kanvis: feature-by-feature comparison (Phase Three)

*Prepared 14 October 2026 on branch `kanvis-reaudit` (off `origin/main` at `dafb730`). Updated 15 October 2026 after Waves S1 to S8: PRs #39 and #42 to #44 merged, with side PRs #40 and #41; PR #45 (S8) is in review, merging 15 Oct. For the founder (Nadine), Paul, the ITS team and the engineers who will act on it.*

This document compares every part of Recruiting OS with the matching part of Kanvis (kanvis.me), one feature at a time. It is built only from the verified audit of our own code (`docs/audit/`), the Kanvis research of 7 October, the re-check of 14 October and the candidate-side application of 15 October [B15]. It does not repeat decisions you already made on 7-13 October; where one of those decisions covers a row, the row says so.

Three facts frame everything below:

- **Kanvis's candidate side has now been seen working.** On 15 October you applied to a real employer's robotics trainer job from your own Kanvis candidate account and answered every question yourself: the details filled from your profile, the company's questions, the AI interview, review and send, then the score. Kanvis had not changed between 7 and 14 October.
- **We still have no Kanvis recruiter account.** Recruiter access is waitlist-only, so the recruiter side is still known only from the public demo and from text inside Kanvis's shipped code (INFERRED).
- **Most of this comparison has been acted on, and our app is in good health.** Eight waves (S1 to S8) went in on 14 and 15 October, and the five first-batch decisions are made. After S8: 249 test files, 2,849 tests passed, 6 skipped, none failed; TypeScript clean in all 15 projects; the build passes; 37 browser journeys passed at S7. The latest real-model fairness run (15 Oct, after S7, run 38717936154, about $0.42) passed all 11 résumé-reading and scoring suites it ran; the run after S5 had failed two bias pairs, fixed in S7. The screening-chat tests have run only on the stand-in AI so far. The `tests/web` folder is still not type-checked in CI (bug 135).

### What the comparison says, in one paragraph

*Updated 15 October 2026.* The Kanvis ideas you chose on 7 October were built first, and eight waves on 14 and 15 October went further. The app now looks and flows like Kanvis where it matters: a short menu, a Jobs list with filters and badges, a ten-step quick start, a LinkedIn post to copy, and, since S7, the Kanvis way of applying. The résumé fills the form, the company asks its own questions, and a chat digs into the gaps. The answers are written up for the candidate to check before Send, and afterwards the candidate sees their score and what they sent. The serious bugs the audit found are fixed: allowances, scheduling, deletion, permissions, steadier scoring and outages. The résumé-reading and scoring fairness tests pass on the real models; the screening-chat tests have not yet run there. We are still far ahead of Kanvis wherever trust and operations matter: a real ITS recruiter on demand, interview scheduling, approvals, job boards, compliance rules, privacy requests and audit history. Kanvis is still ahead on a candidate product across companies (accounts, public profiles, an AI "twin", résumé tools, a job board), which we leave out by choice. It is also ahead on a few team conveniences (moving many people at once, keyboard navigation, richer reports, adding people who never applied), which wait for later decisions.

### The 10 most important overlaps and gaps (found 14 October; where each stands on 15 October)

5. **Changing a role's must-haves does not re-read anyone**, so old scores no longer match the role (CMP-080). D-05. **Now:** not now.
6. **Every new customer is stuck on Trial.** Monthly allowances never reset, and ITS has no screen to move a customer to another plan (CMP-014, CMP-015, CMP-141). Fix first; no decision needed. **Now:** fixed in S2.
7. **Interview scheduling, our biggest advantage over Kanvis, has the most serious bugs**: rescheduling reuses the old dates so no times can be offered, saving availability does not wake waiting interviews, and a candidate whose booked time is moved is not told (CMP-125). 8. **A candidate who asks to be deleted stays in the AI readings and the AI log** (CMP-142, CMP-164). Fix first. **Now:** fixed in S2 and S3.
9. **Interviewers and narrowly-scoped ITS recruiters can see more than they should through some addresses** (CMP-010, CMP-011, CMP-134). Fix first. **Now:** fixed in S4.
10. **Kanvis has richer reports and a few hiring-team conveniences we lack**: a visit-to-application funnel, conversion by source (we only count applications per source), comparisons across roles, moving many people at once, a reason on every move, keyboard next and previous, a career timeline and a shareable comparison (CMP-085, CMP-087, CMP-095, CMP-103, CMP-137, CMP-139).
