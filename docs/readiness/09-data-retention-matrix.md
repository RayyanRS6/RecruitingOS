# Data-retention matrix (proposed; counsel decision pending)

Status: **Blocked (counsel)**. These are the proposed conservative defaults from `docs/spec/05` §6 and register items 6 and 9. No automatic deletion is implemented or will be enabled until counsel approves this matrix; anonymization runs only per request after a recorded counsel decision (`docs/runbooks/privacy-requests.md`).

| # | Category | Examples | Proposed retention | Proposed action at end | Exceptions and holds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application decision records | stage history, eligibility results, human decisions with reasons | 4 years from the decision | anonymize identity, keep the record | litigation hold; hired persons become employment records (customer's) |
| 2 | Candidate identity and contact | name, email, phone, location | 4 years after last activity, or on request | anonymize | as above |
| 3 | Resumes and extracted text | file, text, extraction facts | 4 years after last activity, or on request | delete file and text | litigation hold |
| 4 | Screening transcripts and answers | text conversation, structured answers | 4 years, or on request | delete content, keep counts | litigation hold |
| 5 | AI assessments and runs | evidence-bound assessments, `ai_runs` metadata and cost | 4 years (assessments), 2 years (run logs) | assessments anonymized with the candidate; run logs deleted | audit needs |
| 6 | Communications | emails and texts sent and received | 4 years, or on request | body replaced, envelope kept | litigation hold |
| 7 | Interviews and calendar references | times, participants, event ids | 4 years | keep without identity | none |
| 8 | Audit records | who viewed or changed what, secret reads | 7 years | never altered; identity fields point to anonymized records | none |
| 9 | Events (domain log) | every fact the system recorded | 7 years | as audit | none |
| 10 | Privacy-request records | requests, verification, notes, resolution | 7 years | keep | none |
| 11 | Account and team data | users, memberships, sessions, invitations | life of the account plus 2 years; sessions 30 days | delete | none |
| 12 | Private team notes on a candidate (`application_notes`, plan H3) | a team member's own note, the prompt it started from, its earlier versions, removed notes | as row 1: 4 years from the decision, or on request | delete the words (body and earlier versions); keep who wrote a note and when | litigation hold; left out of a data-access export and deleted on anonymization until counsel answers register item 25 |
| 13 | Interview follow-ups (`interview_followups`, the Cancelled column; migration 0035) | the choice a candidate made after cancelling, or that they did not come; the automatic new times and how they ended | 4 years, as row 7 | keep without identity; no words are stored (the candidate's own reason stays on the interview, row 7) | none; in a data-access export as `interviewCancellations` (the choice and when) |
| 14 | Locked applications (`applications.locked_at`; migration 0036; the plans of 5 November 2026) | an application past a plan's candidates per job: the same data as rows 2, 3 and 6, never read by AI or the team while locked; `application.locked` and `application.unlocked` in events and audit (position, limit, plan, cause, never a name) | the same as every applicant: 4 years, counted from last activity because no decision exists to count row 1 from; the events and audit lines as rows 8 and 9 | as rows 2, 3 and 6; nothing is deleted automatically, and a withdrawal or deletion frees no place | litigation hold; if a Free company leaves, they stay with ITS until counsel answers, because the employer may still have a duty to keep applicant records; ITS can produce them for a recorded legal reason, without an upgrade. In a data-access export with `hiringTeamCanSeeThis` and, once opened, `hiringTeamCanSeeThisSince`, in no plan's words |
| 15 | Requests for access, waiting or declined (`SIGNUP_MODE=approval`; 6 November 2026) | what someone told us before ITS let them in: the company row (name, careers address, kind of business, size, time zone, locations, website), the membership `requested` or `declined`, their account (verified email and name), `company.access_declined` in events and audit; outside the database, the emails at the email provider and ITS's copy in the queue mailbox | not decided (register item 35). Proposed: 2 years from the decline, or on request; a request nobody answered has no end yet | proposed: delete the company row, the membership and the account if it has no other membership; the events and audit lines as rows 8 and 9; nothing is deleted automatically | the row is what lets ITS undo a Decline. Not a candidate, so the privacy console can record a request but not carry it out (`docs/runbooks/privacy-requests.md`) |

Open counsel questions (register `11`): the applicable federal and California periods for applicant records of employers of 10 to 150 people; whether anonymization satisfies a deletion request where a retention duty applies; treatment of hired candidates; litigation-hold procedure; the 45-day response deadline and its extension; whether a candidate's access request must include the team's private notes and whether a deletion request must delete them (register item 25: the built defaults are "left out of the export" and "deleted on anonymization"); who holds locked records, for how long, whether the employer can reach them for a legal need without paying, and whether keeping them as a reason to upgrade is allowed for a service provider (register item 31); what we keep about a declined request for access, for how long, and how the person asks for it to go (register item 35).
