# Eligibility-question policy

1. **What an eligibility question is.** A yes/no question about a lawful, job-related requirement the employer stated during intake: a required license or certification, legal authorization to work, minimum age where required by law, availability for the stated schedule, ability to perform an essential function with or without reasonable accommodation.
2. **What is never asked.** Questions about protected characteristics (age beyond a legal minimum, race, religion, national origin, disability, pregnancy, genetic information, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity, and any other protected class), salary history where prohibited, criminal history before a conditional offer where prohibited, or any proxy for these. The intake assistant refuses to add such criteria and explains why (scenario step 1b).
3. **How answers are used.** A "no" answer to a required eligibility question places the application on hold for the hiring team's review. It never declines the candidate automatically. The team may waive the requirement, ask the candidate to clarify, or decline with a recorded reason; a decline message goes out only after that human decision (correction 6).
4. **Candidate wording.** Each question is shown in plain language with the reason it is asked ("This role requires an active California RDH license").
5. **Accommodation.** Every candidate page and the scheduling page invite the candidate to tell us what would make the process easier; requests are routed to the hiring team and recorded.
6. **Records.** The questions shown, the wording version, and the answers are stored with the application; changes to a role's questions create a new packet version with the approver recorded.
