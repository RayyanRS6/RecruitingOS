# Human-review and rejection policy

1. **Only a person declines.** No candidate is rejected by software at any stage, for any reason, including a failed eligibility answer, a low assessment, an unanswered screening, or an expired interview offer. Those situations hold the application, notify the team, remind, and escalate to an ITS recruiter if ignored. The timer is operational; it is not permission for adverse action.
2. **Every decline has a recorded reason** chosen by the person from a fixed list (experience, skills, pay expectations, location or schedule, position filled, other with a note), the person's identity, and the time. The candidate message goes out only after the decision is recorded, uses one of the approved wordings (never AI-written; a person may add one checked personal line), and is sent by a person when they choose (decisions are private until then; the person can undo a Send for 30 seconds). If nobody has told the candidate five working days after the decision, the standard message goes by itself under the default setting; where approval is required, a person keeps decision messages, or an ITS recruiter runs the role, a recruiter is brought in instead and nothing is sent. When a role closes, the people still in process get one closing message approved once for all of them. Candidates in Colorado are told they may ask for the reasons and a person's review (counsel register items 23 and 24). When several people are filed as not moving forward together in Review and apply, each still gets their own reason from the fixed list, pre-filled from that person's own evidence (for example a must-have their own words show is missing), which the person applying can change before applying and which is recorded under that person's name with the assistant's suggestion beside it (counsel register items 21 and 22).
3. **What software may do on its own** (with the customer's policy settings, never below the minimums): acknowledge, extract, assess with evidence, ask approved screening questions, recommend a review, schedule an approved interview, send approved operational messages, remind.
4. **What always needs a person's approval or action:** approving a role packet, inviting to a screen (where the customer sets it), requesting an interview (never below approval), deciding after an interview, declining, any offer or compensation step, any adverse action, changing eligibility criteria.
5. **Human review on request.** A candidate can ask for a person at any time (HUMAN, the status page); the request becomes an ITS recruiter escalation with a business-hours deadline.
6. **Evidence and explanation.** Assessments quote the candidate's own words and resume; claims without evidence are labelled as unknown; the team sees the evidence and a 0-100 match score worked out by code from it (never written by the AI), with the breakdown of what cost points shown wherever the number appears; missing information is set aside and never counted against anyone, and a person decides (counsel register item 21).
7. **Records.** Decisions, reasons, approvals, and overrides are events on the application's timeline and in the audit log, retained per the retention schedule.
