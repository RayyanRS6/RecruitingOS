# Human-review and rejection policy

1. **Only a person declines.** No candidate is rejected by software at any stage, for any reason, including a failed eligibility answer, a low assessment, an unanswered screening, or an expired interview offer. Those situations hold the application, notify the team, remind, and escalate to an ITS recruiter if ignored. The timer is operational; it is not permission for adverse action.
2. **Every decline has a recorded reason** chosen by the person from a fixed list (experience, skills, pay expectations, location or schedule, position filled, other with a note), the person's identity, and the time. The candidate message goes out only after the decision is recorded and follows the approved template.
3. **What software may do on its own** (with the customer's policy settings, never below the minimums): acknowledge, extract, assess with evidence, ask approved screening questions, recommend a review, schedule an approved interview, send approved operational messages, remind.
4. **What always needs a person's approval or action:** approving a role packet, inviting to a screen (where the customer sets it), requesting an interview (never below approval), deciding after an interview, declining, any offer or compensation step, any adverse action, changing eligibility criteria.
5. **Human review on request.** A candidate can ask for a person at any time (HUMAN, the status page); the request becomes an ITS recruiter escalation with a business-hours deadline.
6. **Evidence and explanation.** Assessments quote the candidate's own words and resume; claims without evidence are labelled as unknown; the team sees the evidence, not a score.
7. **Records.** Decisions, reasons, approvals, and overrides are events on the application's timeline and in the audit log, retained per the retention schedule.
