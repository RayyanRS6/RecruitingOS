# Proposed retention schedule (for the pilot agreement, Exhibit E)

| Data | Proposed period | End-of-period action |
|---|---|---|
| Application decision records (stages, eligibility results, decisions with reasons) | 4 years from the decision | identity removed; record kept |
| Candidate identity and contact details | 4 years after last activity, or sooner on request | removed |
| Resumes, extracted text, screening transcripts, messages | 4 years, or sooner on request | deleted; counts and envelopes kept |
| Interview records | 4 years | kept without identity |
| Audit and event logs | 7 years | kept |
| Privacy-request records | 7 years | kept |
| Account data (users, memberships) | life of the account plus 2 years | deleted |
| Applications locked by a plan's limit (since 5 November 2026): as the rows above, with no decision to count from | 4 years after last activity, or sooner on request | as the rows above; nothing automatic; a deletion frees no place |

Holds: litigation hold, a candidate who was hired (employment records belong to the employer), or a dispute the employer reports suspend deletion until counsel decides.

[Counsel: confirm periods against federal (Title VII, ADEA, ADA one-year rules, EEO-1 where applicable) and California requirements for employers of 10 to 150 people; advise whether 4 years is appropriate as a conservative default and whether anonymization satisfies deletion duties. Since 5 November 2026, also: who holds locked records when a company leaves, whether the employer can reach them for a legal need without paying (proposed: ITS produces them for a recorded legal reason), and whether the employer's duty to keep applicant records reaches applications it never saw (item 31).]
