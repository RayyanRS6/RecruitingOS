# Free plan terms for testers

Prepared 6 November 2026 by the production team as input for counsel and the founder (`docs/spec/11` items 15 and 32). The pilot agreement (`01`) is for one design partner and terms of service are not written, while the plans of 5 November 2026 (`docs/workflow-decisions.md`) let companies ITS lets in use the product on Free. This is the short agreement recommended for each tester to sign before a real job. Risk R-LEGAL-1 applies: nobody signs this for real use until the founder has accepted the risk again in writing, or counsel has answered (`docs/spec/00` section Q). Every choice the founder must make is in [square brackets].

Between **Identified Talent Solutions** ("ITS") and **[Tester's legal name]** ("Tester"), effective on the date of the last signature.

## 1. What this is

ITS lets Tester use its recruiting software on the Free plan, to try it, because ITS accepted Tester's request or added Tester's address. Tester is the employer of anyone it hires. A person at ITS decides who is let in, may decline a request, and may end this as section 8 says. [Founder: ending it cannot be done from the console today. ITS can take Tester's public pages offline, and taking its access away needs an engineer.]

## 2. What the Free plan includes

One job, ever: once it has gone live it counts for good, even after it closes. Tester's team can read the first [5] people who apply to that job, in the order they applied. Up to [2] people on Tester's team, [1] recruiter request a month, and a limited amount of AI help for the life of the company, with no second allowance. Tester's team posts the job on outside job boards from its own accounts. ITS may change these numbers for a later change of plan; the limits Tester already has do not change until ITS applies a plan to Tester's company. No fee and no card.

## 3. People past the limit

A job on Free keeps taking applications past that number. Those applications are locked: Tester's team sees that they exist and when they came, and nothing else, by any route. No AI reads them. The order people applied in decides this, and nothing else. Someone who withdraws or is declined does not free a place. ITS's software tells each of them that the application is on file, that Tester may not be able to review every application, and that they will hear by email when the job closes or at the latest 30 days after applying, and tells them that if it has not been reviewed. Nothing tells them about Tester's plan. They keep every right they have over their information, and a person at ITS answers them. Tester's team is not told who they are or what they asked.

[Counsel: whether this is permitted at all, and what Tester must be told, agree to or do: items 27 to 31. If the answer is no, this section becomes a plain stop: the job takes no more applications past the number.]

## 4. Moving to another plan

Tester asks from the product. No card is charged, ITS contacts Tester, and nothing changes until ITS changes the plan. A plan's price and how it is paid are agreed separately in writing [form to be chosen]. A move to a bigger plan opens locked people, oldest first, up to the new number. A move to a smaller plan never locks anyone Tester's team could already read, and new applicants follow the new number.

## 5. Decisions stay with Tester

The software organizes information and suggests. It does not reject anyone. Tester's people make every decision to advance, hold or decline, and Tester remains responsible for employment, anti-discrimination and pay-transparency law where it hires. [Counsel: automated-decision duties, as in the pilot agreement's section 6, and whether locking is itself such a decision (item 27).]

## 6. Where Tester may not use it

Roles and candidates in the United States only, and in English. [Until counsel has answered items 20 and 21: no job or candidate in New York City, Illinois, California or Colorado, and no remote job.] [With made-up applicants only, until the founder tells Tester otherwise in writing.]

## 7. Data

Tester decides the purposes of processing candidate data, and ITS processes it on Tester's behalf to provide the software. The candidate privacy notice (`02`), the AI-use disclosure (`03`), the subprocessor list (`09`), the privacy-request process (`08`) and the proposed retention schedule (`10`) apply as drafted. If Tester stops using the software, ITS keeps records, locked ones included, on that schedule, and can produce them for a recorded legal reason. [Counsel: service-provider terms; who holds locked records; whether keeping them as a reason to upgrade is allowed; item 31.]

## 8. Status of the software

It is new and offered "as is" for evaluation, with no promise of any number of applicants, any quality or any hire. ITS may change it or end this at any time on [14] days' written notice, and will tell Tester about anything that affects candidates in progress. [Counsel: disclaimers, a liability cap and carve-outs.]

## 9. General

Tester owns its data and ITS owns the software. Feedback may be used to improve the software. Governing law [California]. Amendments in writing.
