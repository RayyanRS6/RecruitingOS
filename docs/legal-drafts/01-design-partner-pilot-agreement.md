# Design Partner Pilot Agreement

Between **Identified Talent Solutions** ("ITS") and **[Design Partner legal name]** ("Partner"), effective on the date of the last signature.

## 1. Purpose and scope
ITS provides Partner with access to its recruiting software and recruiter support (the "Service") for the purpose of evaluating the Service on real hiring needs during a private pilot. The Service includes: role definition assistance, a hosted careers page and application form, candidate acknowledgment and screening assistance, evidence-based candidate summaries, interview scheduling, and access to ITS recruiters on request.

## 2. Term
The pilot runs for [90] days from the effective date and may be extended by written agreement. Either party may end it on [14] days' written notice. Sections 5 to 9 survive.

## 3. Fees
No fee is charged during the pilot (the "Design Partner" plan: up to three active roles, unlimited seats). ITS may propose paid plans at the end of the pilot; nothing obliges Partner to buy.

## 4. What Partner agrees to do
Provide honest feedback at agreed intervals; ensure its users hold accounts in their own names; make hiring decisions itself (Section 6); post jobs on external boards only through its own accounts where the Service cannot post on its behalf.

## 5. Data protection and roles
Partner is the employer and decides the purposes of processing candidate data; ITS processes candidate data on Partner's behalf to provide the Service. ITS will: process candidate data only to provide the Service and as Partner instructs; keep the security controls described in Exhibit B; use the subprocessors in Exhibit C and give [30] days' notice of changes; assist Partner with candidate privacy requests through the process in Exhibit D; notify Partner of a personal-data incident affecting its candidates within [one business day / 72 hours, counsel to advise]; return or delete candidate data at the end of the pilot subject to Exhibit E (retention). [Counsel: confirm whether a separate data processing addendum is required under CCPA/CPRA for a service provider and whether Partner is a "business" under the applicable thresholds.]

## 6. Decisions stay with Partner
The Service organizes information, extracts evidence from candidate materials with quotations, asks screening questions Partner approved, drafts recommendations, and sends operational messages Partner approved. The Service does not make hiring decisions, does not automatically reject candidates, does not change eligibility criteria, does not set compensation, does not send offers, and does not take adverse action. Partner's users make every decision to advance, hold, or decline a candidate, and the Service records who decided and why. ## 7. Candidate notices and consent
Partner authorizes ITS to display the candidate privacy notice, the AI-use disclosure, and the SMS consent language (Exhibits F to H, as approved by counsel) on Partner's behalf, and to send candidate messages that identify Partner's hiring team as the sender from an ITS-operated address.

## 8. ITS recruiter access
When Partner asks for a recruiter, ITS staff receive access limited to that request's role and candidate for a limited time, and every page they open is recorded for Partner (Exhibit D-2, Recruiter-access summary).

## 9. Confidentiality, intellectual property, and feedback
Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential. Partner owns its data; ITS owns the Service. Partner grants ITS a non-exclusive right to use feedback to improve the Service. ITS will not name Partner publicly without consent.
