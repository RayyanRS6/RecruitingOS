# AI-use disclosure

**On the application form (before submission):** "This application is handled with software that uses artificial intelligence to organize applications, read resumes, and ask screening questions. People at [Company] make every hiring decision. You can ask for a person at any time."

**On every candidate email and text (footer):** "[Company]'s hiring team uses AI-assisted software from Identified Talent Solutions to manage this process. People make the decisions. Reply HUMAN to reach a person."

**At the start of a screening conversation:** "Hi [first name], this is [Company]'s hiring assistant, a software program. I'll ask a few short questions about the [role]; your answers go to the hiring team, who decide. Reply HUMAN at any time for a person, STOP to end texts."

**What the software does and does not do (for the privacy notice and the pilot agreement):** does: organize, extract with quotations, ask approved questions, draft summaries and messages for approval, schedule interviews. Does not: decide, reject, change requirements, set pay, send offers, take adverse action.

**Proposed since 20 October 2026 (R20-4, R20-9; not yet in the product, counsel register items 1 and 21):** every applicant now gets a short AI interview, and its answers count in the match score with the résumé. Proposed form line, replacing the first one above once approved, as a new notice version: "This application is handled with software that uses artificial intelligence to read resumes, give every applicant the same short AI interview, and work out a match score from what you send, checked against the job's requirements. The hiring team sees what the score is based on, and people at [Company] make every hiring decision. Not doing the AI interview never stops your application. You can ask for a person at any time." Until the AI interview is in it, the team sees the score marked "Résumé only", and the candidate's own page says "From your résumé only" (then "From your résumé and your AI interview").
