# Interviews, scheduling, cancellations and the interview loop

Audit of branch `kanvis-reaudit` (= origin/main `dafb730`, 14 Oct 2026). Read-only. Source, migrations and tests were treated as the truth; docs were used as leads. Tests were not run by this agent. All paths are relative to `recruiting-os/`.

## 1. Summary

The interview area is the most complete and most heavily tested part of the product's back half. A person on the hiring team (owner, admin, hiring manager, or an ITS recruiter under a grant) approves every interview from the candidate's page; nothing schedules on its own. A background workflow (`schedule_interview`) then works out times from each interviewer's declared hours (or a connected Google Calendar), emails the candidate a link, books the chosen time, sends a confirmation with a calendar file, reminds both sides 24 hours and 2 hours before, closes the interview an hour after it ends and asks "Did it take place?" and "How did it go?". The decision afterwards (next round, finalist, hire, not moving forward) is always a person's and is recorded with a reason; a decline stays private until someone sends the decision message.

The candidate side is a set of server-rendered pages reached by a token link: pick a time in their own time zone, say "none of these work", ask for an accommodation, download the calendar file, reschedule twice on their own, and cancel. The October "Cancelled column" work (migration 0035) is fully wired: a candidate who cancels must choose "send me new times later" or "I'm no longer interested", and a candidate the team marks as a no-show goes into the same column labelled No-show. People who asked for new times get exactly one automatic set after a company-chosen number of working days (default 3, 1 to 10, or off), with many guards (ITS company check, recruiter takeover, a HUMAN or "no thanks" reply, hold, both reschedules used, deletion). The team can send new times to one person or to the whole column.

Calendar integration is honest about its state: there is a real Google Calendar adapter (OAuth with PKCE, free/busy, idempotent events, Meet links, revocation), but it has only been exercised against a scripted fake HTTP layer, and the documented staging and worker configuration sets `CALENDAR_PROVIDER=fake`, which hides the "Connect Google Calendar" button. In practice every interview today runs on declared hours plus emailed `.ics` files. There is no Microsoft/Outlook calendar. Interview recording, transcripts, scorecards and debriefs are not built (tables and policy rows exist, nothing writes them), as expected.

The main problems found are in the less-travelled scheduling paths: rescheduling reuses the original two-week scheduling window (so a late reschedule or late interviewer availability can leave nobody able to book, with no way in the UI to widen the window); the "three collisions" escalation counts every reschedule, so it can fire after one collision; a candidate's pick or a "Book this for them" click is silently lost when the workflow is not waiting for it; saving availability does not wake an interview stuck on "no common times"; a team reschedule of a booked interview never tells the candidate their booked time is off; after "none of these work" any new times are never emailed (the times email is de-duplicated per attempt); and an interviewer can read every interview on a role through an unused API route.

## 3. Workflows traced

### W1. Approve, schedule, book, remind, close (the main path)
Actor: owner or hiring manager, then the candidate, then interviewers.
1. UI: on the candidate panel the person opens "Approve an interview" (`needs-you.tsx` InterviewStep). It is pre-filled from GET `/api/v1/applications/:id/interview-draft` (`routes/interviews.ts` L338 → `interviewDraftFor` L310): next round of the approved packet, the plan's interviewers who are still active, link source, and the default window (next 10 business days, `defaultSchedulingWindow`, `packages/core/src/scheduling.ts` L180).
2. UI → API: "Approve and offer times" posts `{roundKey, interviewerUserIds, linkSource, meetingUrl?, invitationLine?}` to POST `/api/v1/applications/:id/interviews` (L278). Requires `interview.request`. A personal line is checked by `checkPersonalLine`.
3. Service: `createApprovedInterview` (`interview-create.ts` L52) locks the application, checks the stage is one of `INTERVIEW_FROM` (L12), that the round is in the packet, every interviewer is an active member, and no other interview is open. It inserts `interviews` (status `proposed`) and `interview_participants` (status `invited`), notifies the interviewers, resolves any pending "assistant suggests an interview" approval, moves the application to `interview_recommended` (or ends a private mark), emits events, and starts the `schedule_interview` run.
4. Workflow `prepare` (`schedule-interview.ts` L133): gathers each interviewer's constraints (`constraintsFor`, calendar if a live connection, else declared hours, else none). If nobody has availability it notifies the team and the interviewers and waits for `availability.updated` (48h loops).
5. `propose_slots` (L151): `computeSlots` intersects free time over the window (2 per day, up to 8, at least 24h out). With no common time it notifies "No common times" and waits 48h. Otherwise writes `offered_slots`, status `scheduling`.
6. `offer_slots` (L168): issues a 14-day `schedule_token`, sends the "Pick a time" email through the outbox (`sendSlotsOffered`; held if ITS has not confirmed the company), emits `interview.slots_offered`, waits 48h for `<id>:interview.slot_selected` or `<id>:interview.none_work`.
7. Candidate: opens `/c/schedule/:token` (`schedule.tsx` L151), picks a time, optional accommodation note; POST (L206) stores `selected_slot`, candidate time zone, notifies the team about an accommodation, signals the run.
8. `book` (L235): re-checks the slot against fresh busy time; on a collision re-proposes (attempt +1). Otherwise locks the application, writes `starts_at/ends_at`, creates calendar events for connected interviewers (Google only), sets status `scheduled`, participants `confirmed`, moves the application to `interview_scheduled`.
9. `confirm` (L268): candidate confirmation email with both time zones and `.ics`; in-app notices; each interviewer gets an email with the brief (pay redacted unless their role may see pay).
10. `on_timer` (L289): candidate email reminders at 24h and 2h before (skipped inside the last 45 minutes), interviewer in-app reminders.
11. `close` (L320): one hour after the end, status `completed`, application to `interview_complete`, interviewers asked "Did it take place?", team asked "How did it go?".
12. Data written: `interviews`, `interview_participants`, `workflow_runs`, `jobs`, `communications` (keys `slots:`, `slots_reminder:`, `confirm:`, `iv_reminder:`, `iv_confirm_int:`), `notifications`, `events`, `audit_records` (credential reads).

### W2. "None of these work"
1. Candidate: POST `/c/schedule/:token/none-work` with a note (`schedule.tsx` L229) stores `candidate_availability`, emits, signals `<id>:interview.none_work`.
2. `after_offer` (L202-205) notifies the team ("Widen the window or free some time; new times go out as soon as they exist") and waits for `<id>:window.updated` or `<id>:availability.updated`, 48h.
3. Nothing in the code emits either key with the interview id (see bugs). After 48h `propose_slots` recomputes over the same window (usually the same times). Even when the times do change, the "Pick a time" email is never sent again: it reuses the key `slots:<id>:<attempt>` and the attempt has not moved, so the outbox returns the earlier message (`packages/agents/src/outbox.ts` L181-182). The candidate hears only the reminder (if it was not already sent) and then the expiry (bug 23).
4. The team's only levers in the UI: "Offer new times" (same window and interviewers, `Interviews.tsx` L196) or cancel and approve a new interview.

### W3. Candidate self-reschedule
1. Candidate: confirmed page (`schedule.tsx` L252) shows "Ask for a different time" while fewer than 2 reschedules were used.
2. POST `/c/schedule/:token/reschedule` (L335): locks the application, sets status `scheduling`, attempt +1, clears times, increments `reschedule_count_candidate`, keeps the token and the original `scheduling_window`, moves the application back to `interview_recommended`, notifies interviewers, queues calendar-event removal, starts a new `schedule_interview` run.
3. The candidate lands on "We are getting new times ready" until the new offer exists. After two reschedules the button is replaced by "reply and a person will arrange it" and a third press notifies the team (`interview.reschedule_limit`).

### W4. Team reschedule or cancel
1. UI: Interviews page sheet, "Change it" card (`Interviews.tsx` L192-200): reason field, "Offer new times" or "Cancel interview" (confirm).
2. API: POST `/interviews/:id/reschedule` or `/cancel` (`routes/interviews.ts` L182, L192), `interview.manage`. Both go through `supersede` (L75), which locks the application first.
3. Reschedule: status `scheduling`, attempt +1, participants back to `invited`, `interview.rescheduled` event, stage back to `interview_recommended` if it was booked, new run, calendar removal job. The candidate gets only the new "Pick a time" email (first-offer wording) and is not told the old time is off.
4. Cancel: `cancelInterviewInTx` (`interview-cancel.ts` L43) sets `cancelled`, attempt +1, resolves a Cancelled-column entry if this interview carried new times, moves a booked candidate back, notifies interviewers, emails the candidate with the team's reason (and a CANCEL `.ics`) if they had a booked time or offered times, queues calendar removal. The page then offers "Approve a new interview".

### W5. Book on the candidate's behalf
1. UI: "Times offered to the candidate" card, "Book this for them" (`Interviews.tsx` L179-184).
2. API: POST `/interviews/:id/book-on-behalf` (`routes/interviews.ts` L252), `interview.manage`, status must be `scheduling` and the slot must be one offered. Writes `selected_slot`, an `interview.slot_selected` event with `onBehalf`, an audit record, and signals `<id>:interview.slot_selected`.
3. If the run is waiting for that signal, `book` runs as in W1. If not (after "none of these work", during "no common times", or after the collision escalation), nothing books and the next `propose_slots` clears the selection.

### W6. Candidate cancels, goes under Cancelled, one automatic set of new times
1. Candidate: confirmed page shows the cancel form only for a booked interview still ahead, with "Send me new times later" / "I'm no longer interested", an optional reason and a required tick (`schedule.tsx` L306-321). The hint under "new times later" is worked out by `autoNewTimesPlan` so it only promises a day when one will be armed.
2. POST `/c/schedule/:token/cancel` (L392): locks the application, guarded update to `cancelled` (attempt +1), moves the application to `interview_recommended`, reads the plan under a share lock on the company row, writes an `interview_followups` row (`openFollowUp`), arms `interview_new_times` if allowed (`armNewTimes`), notifies the team (with what happens next) and the interviewers, queues calendar removal, emails the candidate a confirmation saying what comes next. Post/Redirect/Get to `/cancelled`.
3. Workflow `interview_new_times` (`packages/workflows/src/definitions/new-times.ts`): `wait` sleeps until the stored due moment (N working days later, 09:00 company time or the start of contact hours; ITS holidays skipped; `packages/agents/src/new-times.ts` L26-48). `send` re-checks everything under the application and follow-up locks; waits up to 10 days for ITS to re-confirm the company if needed; then `reopenForNewTimes` reopens the same interview with a fresh window and starts `schedule_interview` carrying the follow-up. The set is `preparing` until the times email is on its way, then `sent` (`newTimesOffered`, `interview-followups.ts` L340).
4. The person leaves the column when they book (`rebooked`), a person moves them, the team cancels the reopened interview, or they leave the process (`packages/core/src/stages.ts` L198).

### W7. Candidate no-show, then new times for the same round
1. UI: "Candidate did not show" on the interview sheet (`Interviews.tsx` L189).
2. API: POST `/interviews/:id/outcome` with `no_show_candidate` (`routes/interviews.ts` L209): status `no_show`, moves a booked candidate to `interview_complete`, opens a `candidate_no_show` follow-up (never armed) if still at an interview stage, undecided and the role is open; notifies the team "did not come... send new times or decide".
3. Team: "Send new times" (`needs-you.tsx` CancelledCard or `Interviews.tsx` L201) → POST `/applications/:id/new-times` (`routes/new-times.ts` L19) → `sendNewTimes` (`services/new-times.ts` L65): refuses without email, before the ITS check, or with times already out; a no-show is not reopenable, so it needs `interview.request` too and creates a new interview for the same round with the same active interviewers (`createApprovedInterview`, which marks the missed interview decided `new_times`). Times email uses the "new times" opening.

### W8. Send new times to several people
1. UI: Talent page, Cancelled tab, "Send new times…" sheet (`People.tsx` L246-251, L439-485): asked-for-new-times and no-shows ticked, "no longer interested" unticked, people with times already out disabled.
2. API: POST `/api/v1/roles/:id/new-times` (L42) → `sendNewTimesBatch` (L116): one transaction per person, each result with a plain reason, one `interview.new_times_batch` event. ITS staff scoped to one candidate reach only that candidate.

### W9. Switching automatic new times off or changing the days
1. UI: Setup, "When a candidate cancels" (`Setup.tsx` NewTimesCard, words in `apps/web/src/cancelled.ts` L219-260); owners and admins only, recent sign-in required.
2. API: PATCH `/api/v1/companies/current` with `settings.newTimesAfterCancel` (`company-settings.ts` L121-143): switching off while people were promised a day returns 409 `pending_choice_needed` until the owner chooses keep or stop; "stop" ends each pending set (`switched_off`) and cancels its timer. Changing the days never moves anyone already promised a day.

### W10. Connect or disconnect a calendar
1. UI: Setup, "My availability for interviews" (`Setup.tsx` L294-365). The Connect button appears only when the Google provider reports `live`; otherwise the line "Calendar connection is not switched on for this workspace yet".
2. 3. During scheduling, a revoked or failing credential falls back to declared hours, marks the connection "needs attention" and tells the person (`interview-calendar.ts` L63-69).

### W11. Decision after the interview
1. UI: "How did the interview go?" card (`needs-you.tsx` DecisionCard L469): Next round (only if the plan has one and it was not a candidate no-show), Finalist, Hire (opens the hire sheet), Not moving forward (reason from a fixed list).

Records the decision on the newest finished undecided interview; a decline moves to `rejected`, runs the exit clean-up (calls off any upcoming interview with a no-reason note) and leaves the candidate "not told"; next round resolves the Cancelled entry and stops pending automatic times; hire records an outside-the-platform outcome and tells the recruiter.

## 7. Integrations and AI behaviour

Calendars:
- Google Calendar (real adapter `packages/integrations/src/calendar/google.ts`): narrow scopes (events and free/busy), PKCE, refresh token kept only in the secret store, access tokens in memory, idempotent events via a private extended property, Meet link creation, revocation on disconnect. Status: built, not live.
- Fake Google (`fake-google.ts`): in-memory, with a development consent page that returns 404 in production. Used in tests and local development.
- Microsoft / Outlook: not built.
- `.ics` calendar files: built in-house and attached to confirmation and cancellation emails; always available. This is the real calendar path today.

Email: every candidate scheduling message goes through the outbox (Postmark when configured). Candidate emails wait ("held") until ITS confirms the company. No SMS for interviews.

AI:
- "Suggest a line" for the invitation: prompt `message.suggest_line` v1 on task `message.freeform` (`packages/ai/src/prompts/message-line.ts` L39-41), which routes to the standard tier, Claude Haiku 5.5 (`packages/ai/src/routing.ts` L41, L72). It sees only the company name, the job title and the round title, nothing about the candidate (`services/messages.ts` L214-229). Its output passes the same check as a person's own line (no scores, no reasons, no personal characteristics); a failed check gives no suggestion. A person must choose to use it; if kept, the invitation says the assistant helped, and the server re-marks it as AI-helped from the suggestion run id even if the page left the flag out (`interview-create.ts` L59-60).
- Interviewer brief: no model call. It is composed in code from the packet and earlier AI readings of the résumé and screen (findings and quotes), shows no score, and hides pay from roles without pay access. Only answers the candidate actually sent are used.
- Scheduling, outcomes, cancellations and decisions involve no AI. The routing table still has `interview.brief`, `scorecard.draft` and `debrief.compose` entries, unused.

## 9. Bugs and incomplete features

1. High. Rescheduling reuses the original scheduling window. Approval sets a window of the next 10 business days. The team's "Offer new times" keeps it (`routes/interviews.ts` L90-91: it only defaults when dates are missing, which they never are), the candidate's "Ask for a different time" does not touch it (`schedule.tsx` L350), and late availability recomputes inside it (`schedule-interview.ts` L64-67, L155). Once the window is past, `computeSlots` finds nothing (only times at least 24h from now count, `scheduling.ts` L110/L133). Effect: the owner gets "No common times between <old dates>" every 48 hours, the candidate sees "We are getting new times ready" indefinitely, and the UI has no way to widen the window (the API accepts one, no screen sends it). Workaround: cancel and approve a new interview. Only the Cancelled-column reopen refreshes the window (`new-times-reopen.ts` L62-63, whose comment notes the old one can be in the past).
2. Medium. The "three collisions" escalation counts every attempt. `book` compares `interview.attempt + 1` with `MAX_BOOKING_ATTEMPTS = 3` (`schedule-interview.ts` L25, L245-251), but `attempt` also goes up on every team or candidate reschedule, cancel and reopen. After two candidate reschedules, or on any reopened new-times interview, the first taken slot triggers "Three chosen times were taken" and an ITS escalation. Collisions are realistic even without calendars, because two candidates can be offered the same free time by the same interviewer (only booked interviews count as busy, `interview-calendar.ts` L21). The run then ends, leaving the interview in "Waiting on the candidate" with the old times still on the pick page.
3. Medium. Picks and on-behalf bookings are lost when the workflow is not waiting for them. The pick page shows times whenever the interview is `scheduling` with offered times (`schedule.tsx` L161-162), and "Book this for them" works on any `scheduling` interview (`routes/interviews.ts` L260), but the signal only wakes a run waiting on `interview.slot_selected` (`packages/workflows/src/engine.ts` L101-109). After "none of these work", during "no common times", or after the collision escalation, the selection is stored but nothing books it; the next `propose_slots` clears it (`schedule-interview.ts` L163). The owner sees the toast "Booked on the candidate's behalf"; the candidate sees "Booking your time... refresh this page".
4. Medium. Saving availability does not wake interviews stuck on "no common times" or "none of these work". Those waits are keyed per interview (`<id>:availability.updated`, `<id>:window.updated`, `schedule-interview.ts` L159, L205; keys built in `engine.ts` L421), but PUT `/me/availability` signals the bare key `availability.updated` (`routes/interviews.ts` L471) and nothing anywhere emits `window.updated`. Only the 48-hour timer resumes them. Setup's toast says "Any interview waiting on it moves ahead" (`Setup.tsx` L316), true only when nobody had availability at all.
5. Medium. A team reschedule of a booked interview never tells the candidate the booked time is off. `supersede` sends no cancellation (`routes/interviews.ts` L90-102); the next email opens "Good news: ... would like to interview you" (`interview-messages.ts` L70); no CANCEL calendar file is sent, so with declared hours (today's deployment) the old entry stays in the candidate's calendar until they book again. The reason the owner types is labelled "the candidate sees it if they had a time booked or offered" (`Interviews.tsx` L194) but is only used for cancellations. Other interviewers on the panel are not notified either (only the candidate's own reschedule notifies them).
6. Medium. Interviewers can read every interview on a role. GET `/api/v1/roles/:id/interviews` (`routes/interviews.ts` L437-450) has no participant filter, unlike GET `/interviews` (L132). Interviewers hold `role.read` (`permissions.ts` L66), so they can list all candidates' interviews on any role, including stage, decision, the "kept internally" decision note, cancel reasons, accommodation notes and other interviewers' emails. The route is not used by the UI and has no test.
7. Medium. An interviewer with no availability on a mixed panel is assumed free Monday to Friday 9 to 5 and is never asked. `freeRangesForDay` substitutes default windows for `source: none` (`scheduling.ts` L43, L69); the workflow asks only when every interviewer is unknown (`schedule-interview.ts` L141) and ignores the `unconstrained` list (L156). The approval card tells the owner "Times cannot be offered until they add one in Setup; they will be asked" (`needs-you.tsx` L226), which is not what happens.
8. Low. Once Google is live the candidate will receive duplicate invitations: each connected interviewer's event lists the candidate as an attendee with `sendUpdates=all` (`interview-calendar.ts` L97, `google.ts` L97), plus our own `.ics` with a different UID (`interview-messages.ts` L140-141). Only the first event gets the Meet link.
9. Low. The daily interview maximum counts existing interviews by UTC date (`interview-calendar.ts` L30) but checks the interviewer's local day (`scheduling.ts` L71); evening interviews can count on the wrong day.
10. Low. `.ics` escaping: semicolons are "escaped" to themselves (`ics.ts` L26: `.replace(/;/g, ";")`), and `CN=` parameter values are backslash-escaped instead of quoted (L63-64). A company or candidate name with a comma, colon or semicolon can produce a malformed invitation in some calendar apps.
11. Low. "No common times" repeats every 48 hours with no de-duplication, indefinitely, until someone acts (`schedule-interview.ts` L157-159).
12. Low. A first offer whose email is held for the ITS company check still starts the 48h reminder / 96h expiry clock, so it can expire before the candidate ever received it; only the new-times path waits for `company.verified` (`schedule-interview.ts` L178-187 vs L195). Once the offer has expired, the outbox withdraws the held email when ITS confirms the company ("the step it invites to has ended", `packages/agents/src/outbox.ts` L149, L158), so the candidate never receives it and the owner is told the candidate "did not pick".
13. Low. Slots ignore public holidays and the company's chosen business days (only weekends are skipped, `scheduling.ts` L120), while the automatic new-times day does respect ITS holidays (`agents/src/new-times.ts` L26-35).
14. Low. Book-on-behalf is audited as action `candidate.viewed` (`routes/interviews.ts` L265), which mislabels it in the customer's activity history.
15. Low. An outcome of "happened" can later be overwritten (a completed interview still accepts an outcome, L217), while a no-show cannot; the UI hides the card, but the API allows it without a correction trail.
16. Low. The decision API is looser than the screen: it does not require a finished, undecided interview, and "next round" with none picks the plan's first round (`routes/interviews.ts` L374-406).
17. Low. GET `/interviews` loads the newest 300 interviews of the company and then filters to the interviewer's own (L140-150), so an interviewer at a busy company can miss older ones; each row runs four extra queries and the page polls every 15 seconds (`Interviews.tsx` L30).
18. Low. After an interviewer no-show, the approval card proposes the next round rather than the same one (`routes/interviews.ts` L317-320); the owner must change the round by hand. The candidate is not sent any apology automatically. This is deliberate in code (the comment at `routes/interviews.ts` L307-308 says "an interviewer's no-show still counts as over"), so it is a product-design question for the founder more than a defect; it contradicts `docs/spec/06` L92.
19. Low. The candidate can press "Ask for a different time" after the interview has started (until it closes an hour after the end); there is no time check (`schedule.tsx` L335-341).
20. Low (development only). The fake consent page redirects to any `redirect_to` it is given (`routes/integrations.ts` L141); it returns 404 in production.
21. Low. The Google adapter treats every HTTP 403 (including "permission denied") as a retryable rate limit (`google.ts` L66), so a misconfigured calendar retries until the run fails.
22. Low (plausible race). The calendar-removal job reads the interview's current calendar references when it runs (`interview-calendar.ts` L115-143); if a backed-up worker ran it after the candidate had already rebooked, it would remove the new events.
23. Medium (added by verification). After "none of these work", new times are never emailed to the candidate. The candidate's page promises "the team ... will send new times" (`schedule.tsx` L247). When the run wakes and `propose_slots` finds times, `offer_slots` calls `sendSlotsOffered` with the same key `slots:<id>:<attempt>` (`interview-messages.ts` L84) because the attempt only moves on a reschedule, cancel, reopen or collision; `queueCommunication` returns the earlier message for an existing key (`packages/agents/src/outbox.ts` L181-182). The pick page behind the old link does show the new list, but nobody tells the candidate; if the 48h reminder already went (key `slots_reminder:<id>:<attempt>`, L101) they hear nothing at all and the offer expires 96 hours later. Workaround: the team presses "Offer new times" (new attempt, new email). Effect: the candidate waits for an email that never comes; the owner later sees "did not pick a time".

Incomplete or not built: recording and transcripts (INTERVIEWS-35), scorecards and debrief (INTERVIEWS-36), SMS for interviews (INTERVIEWS-37), widening the window or changing interviewers on an existing interview (INTERVIEWS-38), Microsoft calendar (INTERVIEWS-05), real Google Calendar not switched on (INTERVIEWS-04).
