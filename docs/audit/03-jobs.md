# Area audit: company setup, job creation, approval, publishing and distribution

Repo: recruiting-os, branch kanvis-reaudit = origin/main dafb730 (14 Oct 2026). Read-only audit. Source, migrations and tests are treated as the truth; docs are leads only. Paths are repo-relative.

## 1. Summary

This area is the most complete part of the product we looked at. A business owner can sign themselves up, describe a job in plain words (or paste an old ad), answer tap-to-answer question cards, get a full job draft written by the AI, check it, and ask for approval. Approval is always a person: no setting can make it automatic. The role then opens and the job is "published" to a public careers page, a job page with Google structured data, public job feeds, and (by hand) LinkedIn, Indeed and ZipRecruiter. Nothing goes public for a self-signed-up company until ITS confirms it in the admin console. Every route traced from the screen to the API, the service and the database works as described, and most of it has strong API tests.

What is AI and what is rules: the AI (Claude Haiku 5.5 for the chat, Claude Sonnet 5.5 only for writing the full draft) reads the owner's words and writes the draft. Everything that matters legally or for fairness is then redone by code, not trusted from the AI: who set the pay, the pay shown on every ad, the fairness word check (flags), the legal check, the plan limit, the approval rules, the posting states and the "live" check of each listing.

The job boards are not connected by any API. ITS (or the company's own team) posts each job by hand from the company's own board accounts, then records the listing's address; the system fetches that page and calls it "Live and verified" only if it shows the job title. This is honest and fully built, but it means distribution depends on ITS staff doing manual work.

Main problems found (details in section 10): (1) the plan's monthly allowances never reset, so a trial or paying company that uses up its screening conversations once stays "used up" for good, and later applicants wait up to 60 days and are then told it is not going ahead (Design Partner pilots are exempt; the only ways out are a database edit or an ITS plan change to a bigger plan through an API that has no screen); (2) edits saved while a role is paused never take effect and cannot be sent for approval; (3) someone who can approve can add wording the fairness check flags, "keep" it with their own reason and push it live on an open job (two saves in the editor, or one through the advanced editor), with nobody else looking; more broadly, an approver's wording edit to a live job goes live without the named decision maker or a narrowed approver list; (4) several small loose ends (closed roles can still be edited through one route, interviewers can open any role's details by its address, unused settings and columns, an ITS plan-change API with no screen). Several docs are out of date (section 11).

Only fake AI providers and fake URL checks were exercised in tests; nothing here is verified against the real model in production, and dev/staging marks every board address "live" unless the real link checker is switched on.

## 3. Workflows traced step by step

### W1. A company signs itself up and waits for the ITS check (owner, then ITS platform admin)
1. UI: /setup/company (apps/web/src/pages/CompanySetup.tsx) asks company name, owner name, kind of business, size, city and state, time zone (pre-filled from the computer, snapped to 7 US zones by apps/web/src/company-setup.ts snapTimeZone), website. The business-type hint says whether a hiring guide (playbook) drives drafting.
2. API: POST /api/v1/companies (requireUser only; anyone signed in). Body validated by CreateCompany (packages/contracts/src/api/index.ts:44-53; time zone must be a real zone).
3. Service: createCompany (apps/api/src/services/companies.ts:16-49) in platform scope inserts companies (unique slug, settings {website, careersPage.enabled:true}), an owner membership, applies the trial plan (entitlements rows from plans), emits company.created and membership.changed. With COMPANY_CHECK=auto (tests and local only; production refuses it, apps/api/src/env.ts:193) the company is confirmed at once.
4. Then the route starts the stuck_role_detector workflow and emails the ITS queue mailbox with the company details and owner address (tellItsAboutNewCompany; a failed send raises a high-severity admin alert).
5. DB effect: companies.settings has no verifiedAt. Every public surface checks isCompanyVerified on each request: careers page, job page, apply form, logo (apps/api/src/pages/candidate.tsx:126-129), tracked links (apps/api/src/pages/links.tsx:70), feeds and sitemap (apps/api/src/pages/public-feeds.ts:58). Candidate messages are held in the outbox (packages/agents/src/outbox.ts:102).
6. Owner sees on Today: "ITS is confirming your company" with how many messages wait (apps/api/src/services/brief.ts:434-464), on the role page ROLE_WAITS_FOR_CHECK and on the Posting tab POSTING_WAITS_FOR_CHECK (apps/web/src/company-check.ts).
7. ITS: admin console Customers tab (apps/web/src/pages/Admin.tsx:191) calls POST /api/v1/admin/customers/:id/verification with a reason (platform admin only, every admin call audited). verifyCompany writes verifiedAt/verifiedBy in one jsonb statement, releases held messages, emits company.verified, audits, signals waiting publish runs, and notifies owners/admins/hiring managers (always emailed: packages/core/src/notify-groups.ts:31). Feeds cache is reset immediately.
8. Taking it back (unverifyCompany) puts pages and feeds offline at once and holds messages again; roles, candidates and decisions are untouched.

### W2. Start a hire to "Ask for approval" (owner or hiring manager, AI assisting)
1. UI: "Who are you hiring?" on Today (TodayStart.tsx), the rail's Start a hire button or the N key (start-hire.ts), or /roles/new (NewRole.tsx: StartView, then Builder). Only roles holding role.create see it (owner, admin, hiring manager, recruiter); HR and interviewers get a plain refusal.
2. API: POST /api/v1/intake/sessions with the first message (up to 20,000 characters; a long or many-line message is treated as a pasted ad) or fromRoleId (hire again). assertMayDraft also needs compensation.read and refuses an ITS recruiter whose grant is for one role or candidate.
3. Plan room first: if the company has no free active-role place, 402 limit_reached naming the roles using the places, with a "Talk to ITS about more roles" action (intake.ts:138-149). No draft and no AI call happen on a full plan.
4. Service: startIntakeSession inserts intake_sessions (messages, facts, server_state) and emits intake.started. Then runIntakeTurn: a short transaction records the message; if it is a pasted ad, intake.extract (Haiku) reads title, place, basis and pay into facts (never as confirmed pay); the market benchmark is looked up with no transaction open (BLS OEWS live, else the playbook's ITS estimate, never on the wrong basis); intake.turn (Haiku) replies with a short message and up to three cards.
5. Code then overrides the model: server-owned facts (decision maker id, interviewer ids, suggestions) come from the stored row; cards are rebuilt by prepareQuestions (team cards filled with the real team, pay card drawn by the server, any offered answer failing the fairness check dropped, hourly-or-salary asked first, stated deal-breakers offered back as must-haves). Pay counts as set by a person only if the exact figures appear in words the person typed (payTyped, intake-builder.ts:66-70) or were set on the pay card.
6. A first message that is not about a role leaves no draft (status abandoned, notARole).
7. Answering cards: POST /intake/sessions/:id/answers (set/add/remove semantics; typed proxies refused with a reason, recorded in the conversation as "Not used").
8. Write the draft: POST /intake/sessions/:id/draft, allowed once essentials are in, or after one round of answers, or when the owner said "draft it". draftIntake records progress (benchmark, writing, checking, done) that the page polls each second; intake.compile (Sonnet 5.5, high effort, up to 32,000 tokens) writes the draft; finalizeDraft then sets by rule: pay only from a person's confirmation (who, when, how), discloseRange true, decision maker and interviewers from the team, deal-breakers in the owner's words, "Not set yet" assumptions, then the packet guard. Saved to intake_sessions.draft_packet. No role exists yet and nothing is sent.
9. Optional tools on the draft: preview (C9), change by chat (packet.revise, shown field by field, saved only on Confirm), requirements check with one-tap fixes, keep a flagged phrase with a reason, take over (owner/admin), discard.
10. Ask for approval: POST /intake/sessions/:id/request-approval. Refused (409 not_ready, listing what is left in plain words) until there is a written draft up to date with the answers, all six essentials (title, place, pay, reason for hire, who interviews, who decides), pay a person set or confirmed, and no open flag. Plan room is checked again inside the transaction with the entitlements row locked, so two asks at once cannot pass the limit.
11. requestIntakeApproval runs finalizeDraft once more on the words the person saw (never rewriting them), inserts roles (status pending_approval, created_by = the person) and role_packets version 1 (status proposed, source intake_agent, created_by = the intake agent), marks the session compiled, emits role.created, role_packet.proposed and related events, starts the role_approval workflow and records the active_roles use; all commit together.
12. UI: the builder then posts the closing date the person picked (kept only in this browser until now, Builder.tsx:84-86, 167-174; only for people holding role.manage) and goes to the role page. Toast: "Nothing is posted until it is approved."

### W3. Approval to an open role (approver)
1. Worker: role_approval step request_approval (packages/workflows/src/definitions/role-approval.ts:38-85) resolves the named decision maker (approval-deciders.ts resolveDecisionMaker:81-101; only active owners, admins or hiring managers, matched by stored id, email, exact name or a unique single name) and calls gate() for role_packet.approve. The policy can never resolve to automatic or person-only (MIN and MAX levels hold it at approval, policy.ts:16,25); if it did, the run fails rather than open an unapproved role. An approval_requests row is created with a 14-day expiry; the decision maker (or all approvers) is notified.
2. UI: the role page approval card (apps/web/src/pages/RolePlan.tsx:125-143) and the Today card. packetApprovalView (packet-approval.ts:85-120) tells each viewer whether they may decide and why not.
3. API: POST /api/v1/roles/:id/approve (body: version) or POST /api/v1/approvals/:id/decide. Both apply assertMayApprovePacket: role.approve permission; the named decision maker or an owner only; the person who started the role or wrote the version may not approve unless they are the company's only owner (409 self_approval); no open fairness flags (409 packet_flags); the legal check passes (409 legal_check with the missing items). Sending back is reserved the same way but never blocked by flags or self-approval.
4. DB: approval_requests approved, role_packets.approved_by/at stamped, events approval.granted and role.approved, engine signal approval:<id>.
5. Worker: on_decision then open: the version becomes approved, others superseded, the role becomes open with current_packet_version and opened_at; a Colorado or remote role without a closing date gets one 30 days out; a date chosen earlier that has already passed is dropped and the team told. Then publish starts publish_role.
6. Rejected or expired: the role goes back to draft with the note (owner told); "Request approval again" (POST /roles/:id/request-approval) starts a new run.

### W4. Publishing to the channels (system, ITS recruiter or the team)
1. publish_role v3 (publish-role.ts:60-151): stops if the role is not open; waits with no deadline for company.verified if the company is not confirmed yet.
2. For each chosen channel (careers page, Google, email always; boards ticked on the role, else those the role plan names; ZipRecruiter only with the company's own plan): skip rows a person or the outside holds (posted, submitted, live, paused); create the board's own tracked apply link; call the channel adapter.
3. Careers page: ready_to_post with the job page URL, then moved straight to posted_manually and a posting.verify job queued. Google: submitted_integration (a person later marks it found with a note). Email-in: live_verified only if the inbound mail domain is configured, else failed "Inbound mail domain not configured yet". Boards: ready_to_post with numbered manual steps; when ITS posts for the company (default), an ITS posting task per board and one email to the ITS queue.
4. Failures are recorded on the row and retried up to three times, eight hours apart; a channel failure never blocks the role.
5. ITS path: the recruiter claims the task in /recruiter/queue (no access grant, only the job ad, the posting package and the board's apply link), posts from the company's own board account, records the public address (POST /api/v1/escalations/:id/posting/url, https only), or confirms by hand with a note.
6. Team path: Posting tab (apps/web/src/pages/RolePosting.tsx) shows the package to copy and the steps; POST /roles/:id/postings/:channel/url records the address (role.manage).
7. Check: the posting.verify job (container.ts:216-255) fetches the address with the real verifier in production (https only, public hosts only, each redirect checked, 10 seconds, 512 KB) and looks for the job title. Found: live_verified and the ITS task settles. Three misses: failed, with the reason, for a person to retry or confirm by hand. A person's own confirmation is never overwritten by a check already queued.

### W5. Editing a role after it is open (owner, admin, hiring manager, recruiter)
1. UI: Edit on the role page opens RoleEditor (plain fields, skills, advanced JSON for admins and recruiters). Approvers see what a live change does before saving (apps/web/src/publishing.ts liveEditWords).
2. ### W6. Closing date, the accepting switch and pause (owner, admin, recruiter)
1. POST /roles/:id/closing-date: today or later in the company's zone; Colorado and remote roles cannot remove it. For an open or paused role a timer is armed (role_closing_date workflow); every change cancels the old timer.
2. At the end of that day the timer turns accepting off with reason "closing date", emits role.applications_closed and tells the team; nobody is rejected; forms opened earlier may still be sent for 24 hours (sealed "form opened" value, services/closing-date.ts). 3. POST /roles/:id/applications/accepting turns applications off or on by hand (reason recorded; the reserved reason "closing date" is refused); POST /roles/:id/pause and /resume change the role (resume restarts publishing and adds emailed applications held meanwhile).
4. Job page stays up and says why the form is closed; apply routes answer 404; tracked links send people to the job page.

### W7. A candidate finds the job and presses Apply now (candidate)
1. Arrives from a feed, Google, the careers page /careers/:slug (lists open roles only), a board listing via /a/:token (visit counted, source sealed in a cookie, board links land on the form), or a share link (lands on the job page).

POST creates the application with its source; the one-visit screening conversation or the receipt follows (next area).

### W8. Sharing a job (owner, admin, hiring manager, recruiter)

## 6. Roles and permissions specific to this area

- Owner: everything here. Approves roles, except a role they started or a version they wrote when another owner exists. The only role that can override a named decision maker.
- Admin: same permissions as owner (company, members, policies, role.manage, approve) but cannot approve a role they started (the sole-owner exception is for owners only) and cannot override a named decision maker.
- Hiring manager: starts hires, edits packets, approves (not their own), shares jobs, sees pay. Cannot pause or resume, set the closing date, tick boards, record listing addresses, switch applications off, change company settings or AI rules (no role.manage, company.update, policy.manage). The role page shows postings read-only to them.
- HR: reads roles and pay, can close a role with its outcome; cannot draft, edit or approve.
- Interviewer: role.read only; roles list narrowed to roles they interview for; pay redacted everywhere except the posting package (see bug 6); can still open any role by its address.
- Recruiter (the company's own, or ITS under a grant): drafts, edits, manages postings; never approves. An ITS recruiter drafts only under a company-wide grant: a grant for one role or one candidate cannot open or start drafts (intake.ts:27-36).
- ITS platform admin: confirms or un-confirms companies, confirms job-board access, sets the apply flow, changes plans (API only), sets a role's apply mode (with a second factor).
- ITS recruiter on a posting task: no access grant; sees only the job ad, the board's apply link and the posting; records the address, confirms an update or a take-down.
- Candidate: public pages only.

## 7. Integrations and AI behaviour

AI (packages/ai/src/routing.ts; only Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 are allowed):
- intake.extract (Haiku, fast): reads a pasted ad; title, place, type, schedule, basis, pay as stated; the text is data only. Ad pay is never treated as confirmed.
- intake.turn v4 (Haiku): the chat reply and up to three cards; told to decline unlawful criteria with a lawful alternative. Code re-checks every offered option, owns the server facts, and decides pay confirmation.
- intake.compile v5 (Sonnet, high effort, 32,000 tokens): writes the whole draft. Code then replaces pay, decision maker, interviewers, deal-breakers, assumptions and every flag; the model's own flags or "kept" marks are discarded.
- packet.revise (Haiku): change by chat; merged, guarded and confirmed by a person; pay changes recorded as set by that person.
- company.about (Haiku): a few sentences about the company; public only after a person approves.
- Deterministic (no AI): packet guard, readiness, plan room, requirements check, legal check, approval rules, publishing, posting states, link check, closing date, feeds, previews.
- Deadline: every AI call in a request shares the 300-second route deadline with a 30-second reserve (packages/ai/src/runner.ts:22-31; providers/anthropic.ts:23-26,143).

What needs a person: every role approval (cannot be automated), every material change to a live job, pay (only a person sets or confirms it), keeping a flagged phrase (with a reason), the ITS company check, posting on boards, calling a board listing live (fetched check or a person's note), marking Google found.

What the candidate can and cannot see (PACKET_VISIBILITY, role-packet.ts:293-329, and the pages): public are title, places, work model, type, schedule, pay range (minimum and maximum, shown on every ad because the guard forces it, packet-guard.ts:218-222), bonus and benefits text, description, must-have and nice-to-have words, eligibility questions with their reason, interview round titles and lengths, number of conversation questions and minutes, approved company text. Never shown: the private pay ceiling, mid-point, pay sources and market figures, why each requirement matters and how it is checked, skills and weights, evaluation criteria, red flags, deal-breakers, internal notes, decision maker, interviewers, reason for hire, timeline, sourcing, flags and declined requests. The screening AI receives the questions with model answers, skills, must-haves checked in the screen, the posted pay and the process, but not the ceiling, deal-breakers, red flags or internal notes (packages/ai/src/prompts/screen.ts:62-75). Deal-breakers are not given to the triage prompt either (only evaluation criteria and red flags, prompts/triage.ts:44).

External integrations:
- BLS OEWS wage data (packages/integrations/src/comp/bls.ts; on unless COMP_BLS_ENABLED=false), with the playbook's ITS estimate as fallback, labelled as such.
- Job boards: manual only (Indeed, LinkedIn free listing, ZipRecruiter) through the posting package and steps; no board API.
- Google for Jobs: schema.org JobPosting on the job page; indexing is never verified by the system.
- Public feeds: one cross-company feed published under the product name (jobs.xml, jobs.json) and a LinkedIn-format feed that LinkedIn has not approved.
- URL verifier: real fetch in production or with LINK_VERIFIER=real; a fake that answers "found" for every address otherwise (registry.ts:74; verify.ts:280-303).
- Email: Postmark for the ITS queue emails, owner notifications and email-in (email-in needs the inbound domain configured).
