## Fixed in Waves S1 to S4 (branch kanvis-simple, PR #39, merged 15 October 2026)

Checked against the code of commits `f704dac` (S1), `569d69a` and `8eab506` (S2), `0996442` (S3), and `4677766` (S4, the permissions wave). The table below is kept as the audit wrote it, with a status column added on 15 Oct for the high and medium rows; a low row not named in these notes is still open as written. Built and tested against fake providers only, like the rest of the branch.

- **Allowances** (rows 2, 6): monthly allowances start again from zero on the period dates already stored (worked out when read or used, no job and no migration); applicants held only by the allowance go through at the reset; a plan change keeps this month's usage. Settings says when the allowances reset (`usage[key].resetsAt` on GET /companies/current).
- **Plan screen** (row 1, the plan part; row 60, "ITS must call an API to change a plan"): **Change plan** in the console's Customers view, with a reason of five characters or more, audited as `plan.changed` (`GET /admin/plans`, `POST /admin/customers/:id/plan`). The other admin actions in row 1 (break-glass, staff-access ledger, sign a person out, AI run detail, add a suppression) are still API only.
- **Alerts** (row 5; row 39 in part): acknowledging means "seen", no longer "silenced for good": an acknowledgement stands while the condition lasts (a day at most), the condition's end resolves the alert, and the next occurrence raises a new one. **Resolve** in the console (`POST /admin/alerts/:id/resolve`). Critical alerts are also emailed to every platform administrator, once, after the commit (row 39 stays open for the other severities).
- **Interview scheduling** (rows 3, 21, 22, 23, 24, 26, 27, 93): new times are worked out over a fresh window from tomorrow, as long as the old one; saving availability or connecting a calendar wakes interviews waiting on it; other times are emailed after "none of these work"; a team reschedule of a booked time tells the candidate (with a cancelling calendar file) and the other interviewers; only real collisions count towards the three; a pick or **Book this for them** is booked directly when no run waits for it (409 `slot_taken` when the time has gone) and audited as `interview.booked_on_behalf`; an interviewer with no hours is never assumed free 9 to 5 (the owner and the interviewer are asked, then a recruiter after four days).
- **Deletion of AI data** (rows 4, 14, 126): anonymising a candidate also scrubs the AI's readings of them and the AI run log about them, found by application, screen, document and workflow run (only ids, task, model, prompt, tokens, cost, timings and status stay); since Wave S3 it also cancels their workflow runs still going and clears run state, step records and the error copies in run events and queued jobs, keeping only ids, status, step names, codes and timings; a daily clean-up repeats both, so a run that finished after the deletion is caught too. The "your data was deleted" email goes after the commit, is recorded only when sent, and stored files are removed after the commit.
- **Scoring input** (rows 84, 136; row 28 in part): the résumé reading (`triage.assess`) and the screening scorecard (`screen.assess`) read a redacted view (name, email, phone, street address, zip code and web links replaced by placeholders, `packages/agents/src/scoring-view.ts`), and earlier applications and their outcomes are no longer sent (the team still sees them). Quotes shown to the team are mapped back to the candidate's original words. City, state, employers, dates and schools stay by design (founder decision D-04 C).
- **Prompt-injection tag stripping** (rows 31 and 15, both in part): candidate text can no longer close the reading's `<resume>`, `<extraction>` or `<application>` blocks, nor the scorecard's `<transcript>` or `<answers>` blocks (`withoutTag`). The résumé extraction prompt and the screening chat's own turns are unchanged, and no adversarial eval was added.
- **Decline wording** (rows 19, 82, Wave S1): every screen and toast that says a decision is private also says that the standard message goes by itself after 5 working days if nobody sends one, with the date when it is known; the hire sheet's close-role sentence says the same (founder decision D-03 A).
- **Interviewers see only their own roles** (rows 40, 55, 66; rows 7, 10, 25 for interviewers): `roleScopeGuard` answers 404 "Role not found." to an interviewer's read of any role they are not on an interview for, on every route under `/roles/:id` (the role, packets, diff, interview plan, requirements check, timeline, postings, links, volume, preview, board, compare, report, screening, interviews); the roles list and the guard share one rule (`rolesInterviewedBy`), and being taken off an interview's panel closes the role again. On their own role, GET `/roles/:id/interviews` lists only their interviews, and the role page and the Share tab's volume carry no applicant numbers. The job page and its Candidates page say "You are not on this job" or "Candidates are for the hiring team" instead of an error.
- **ITS recruiter grants see only their own** (rows 7, 10, 52, 83): a grant about one candidate gets only that candidate in the role's interviews, the role page's counts, the report (JSON and summary CSV), the Share tab's volume and the Activity tab; the screening chat's figures for the whole role are refused (403 `staff_scope`). POST `/approvals/:id/decide` holds a grant to its role or candidate (403 `staff_scope`), and GET `/approvals`, the role page's waiting requests and the Home cards list only what the grant may decide, so a closing message naming every applicant is not shown under a one-candidate grant, nor a company-wide request under a role grant. A grant for all of the company's hiring is unchanged.
- **Team ranks** (rows 8, 43, 44, 56): signing a teammate out is for owners and admins only (HR 403 `admin_only`, an admin on an owner 403 `owner_only`), ends only the sessions working in this company, and is a **Sign out everywhere** button on Settings > Team; the answer and the audit record say nothing about the person's other companies. Withdrawing an invitation to become an owner or admin, directly or by inviting the same address again at a lower rank, follows the same ranks. The pending invitations list needs `members.manage`. Still open: a session left open in another company can switch back here while the person is a member (needs migration 0040).
- **"No common times"** (row 105): the notice goes once per interview and goes again only when something that could change the answer changes (hours, interviewers, length, attempt, window span); the 48-hour re-check stays silent.
