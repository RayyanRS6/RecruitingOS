## State on 9 October 2026 (branch `kanvis-flow`)

This branch carries out the founder's decisions of 7 October 2026, taken after comparing our hiring flow with a competitor's, built in our own words and our own design: B1 (the conversation in the same visit as applying), B2 (a match score), B3 (a stage strip), B4 (one pass to file a group privately), B5 (one-click pool questions), B6 (decisions are private until a person tells the candidate), B7 (a one-time ITS check before a company goes public), and the recommended answers to Q2 to Q33. The code and docs 06, 07, 12 and `docs/spec/22` refer to them by these names.

Like release 1, everything here is **built on mocks and tested against fake providers. Nothing is production-verified**: no prompt has run on the real model, nothing on this branch has been exercised on staging, and nothing is deployed. The branch adds nine migrations, `0026` to `0034`, all additive; the Cancelled column (below) adds a tenth, `0035`.

### The final polish and review round (9 October)

A review of the whole branch confirmed 38 findings (4 high). All were fixed except the founder questions below. In brief:

- **Candidate truth.** "Check your answers" no longer says "Nothing is sent until you do": it says the hiring team sees the answers only once the candidate sends them (the founder decision below) and that without Send the conversation closes a few days after the last change and the application goes on without the answers. Closing lines no longer promise "you will hear back within a few days". "A person will be in touch" shows one fixed deadline (four business hours from the request), and says so plainly once overdue. "No thanks" opts someone out of future roles only after they were told a decision. A candidate promised a conversation link that never came, because the month's allowance was not topped up, is told after 60 days that it is not going ahead and nothing more is needed, and the owner is told.
- **Decisions.** A decision nobody has told can be taken back with nothing sent. Someone already at the interview step (an interview cancelled, times expired, the next round, "Not now") has an **Approve an interview** card. HR may close a role but is never shown a closing-message Send it cannot use. The AI-help line on a message stays disclosed after small edits. A bounced or stopped decision message puts the person back to "not told", and a retry never delivers a decline that was reversed.
- **Privacy and limits.** Staff never see candidates' live status or confirm-answers links (message bodies are masked), the closing-message preview is refused to a one-candidate grant, and the ITS queue email never names a candidate. A deletion also clears the team's free-text words in outcomes and stage-change reasons. The access-request export separates messages that were sent from those that were not. Each conversation has a cap on model turns, and repeat applications open at most four conversations a day. Allowance counters add in the database, so two screens at once are counted once each. The still-reviewing note never goes to a backlog when first turned on. The active-roles allowance counts the roles using a place now (closing a role frees it at once).
- **Builder and pay.** Market pay is written the way the pay card shows it, places are capitalised, social media and content roles are benchmarked against marketing specialists (13-1161, shown as a rough guide), and ticked must-haves are no longer split into fragments.
- **Founder decisions of 9 October, built the same day.** (1) "Hide them until they send": while a candidate is in the screening conversation and has not pressed Send (or, for an emailed one, until it ends normally), the team and ITS see none of their answers and nothing worked out from them, anywhere (`packages/core/src/screen-answers.ts`); the panel says "Still applying: their answers appear here once they send them". A conversation closed unfinished, sent without, handed to a person or withdrawn stays hidden and is never read: the team sees "Didn't finish the conversation" and the reading and match score are the résumé's. Nothing sends the answers for the candidate: someone who answered everything but never pressed Send gets one reminder and then closes as unfinished like anyone else. The role's figures no longer say where anyone stopped; the reminders about an unsent conversation stay off the Messages tab; a hand-over's reason never says how the assistant thought the candidate sounded; a reading written before that day of an unsent conversation stays hidden even after a recruiter finishes it on a call; and the ITS operations console withholds an AI run's output about an unsent conversation. A recruiter's call notes are shown; the candidate's own data export is unchanged. (2) The 30-day "still reviewing" note is off by default ("Only a person"); a company's saved choice stands, and Setup says how to switch it on.
- **Accessibility and layout.** Undo is reachable by keyboard (an inline Undo where the Send happened, and Tab inside a drawer reaches the toast); focus is kept after Next and Previous, after the last question card, and after a row's stage moves; menus open inside the screen on phones and Tab moves on from an open menu; "Something wrong?" acts only on Enter or a click; scrolling respects reduced motion; the rail says Open, Paused, Waiting or Draft in words wherever it shows no count; Compare has row headers; the chosen note starter is announced. The narrow rail no longer shows a sideways scrollbar.

### What only the founder can do before a deploy

Each is one action at a time, entered by the founder at the provider. No value is written in the repository or in chat.

1. **Apply migrations 0020 to 0025 before deploying.** Run the `migrate` GitHub workflow (Actions, **migrate**, **Run workflow**, pick the environment) or `pnpm db:migrate` from her machine, then deploy. The app and the worker refuse to start while a migration is pending. Procedure and what the output means: `docs/runbooks/release-migrations.md`. The six are additive: 0020 job distribution, 0021 worker heartbeats, 0022 invitations, 0023 reply tokens, 0024 session re-authentication, 0025 lets the two application roles read the migration ledger the boot check compares against (writing it stays the owner's). Staging (5 October 2026): 0018 to 0024 applied through the workflow's `staging` environment; 0025 follows the same way.
2. **The jobs address.** Attach `jobs.identifiedtalent.com` to the Vercel project as a second domain and approve its DNS record when it is shown (gate 2.5). Only then set `JOBS_BASE_URL=https://jobs.identifiedtalent.com` in Vercel and on the Railway worker; until then leave it unset and every job page is served from `APP_BASE_URL`.
3. **The ClamAV service.** Start the `clamav/clamav` service on Railway in the same project as the worker (`docs/runbooks/resume-storage.md`, "Running ClamAV on Railway"), then set `SCAN_PROVIDER=clamav` and `CLAMAV_HOST=<the service's private hostname>` on the worker only. Until then a production worker raises the standing `scanner.fake` alert, and no real résumé may be accepted (`docs/readiness/20` blocker 8).
4. **The Postmark inbound domain.** With the Postmark account and the sending domain in place, add the inbound domain's MX record (shown first), point Postmark's inbound webhook at `/webhooks/postmark/inbound` with the inbound secret, and set `POSTMARK_INBOUND_SECRET` and `EMAIL_INTAKE_DOMAIN` (the real inbound domain) in Vercel and on the worker. This turns on email-in applications and candidate replies (`docs/runbooks/candidate-replies.md`); until then replies reach `EMAIL_REPLY_TO`.
5. **Sentry DSNs.** `SENTRY_DSN` in Vercel and on the worker, `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN` in Vercel if the staff app should report too, `SENTRY_ENVIRONMENT=staging` on the staging Preview; then the scrubbing proof with the test-error route (`docs/readiness/03` section 6).

Without them the production app refuses to start (the worker counts no requests, but once set to `upstash` it needs both values too); if Upstash is unreachable at run time, each process counts on its own and raises an alert.
7. **`ANALYTICS_SALT`.** A different random value per environment, in Vercel and on the worker; production refuses to start without it.
8. **The Railway worker health check.** On the first deploy after the migrations, confirm the worker's deploy passes its `/healthz` check (`railway.json`), its log says `[worker] started`, and the app's `/healthz` shows `worker` with `healthy: true` (`docs/runbooks/railway-worker.md`).
9. **One CI run for the browser specs.** Open or update the pull request so CI runs; Playwright cannot run on the founder's laptop. The run is also the proof for lint, the build and the full suite (previous section).
10. **Optional: the isolation suites against a real test database.** On an empty test database in the staging project, once, as the owner: `GRANT app_tenant, ros_app, ros_worker TO <owner role>`; apply the migrations to it; then run the five suites with `TEST_DATABASE_URL` set to that database's owner string (`docs/readiness/03` section 2 has the exact command and what to record).

## What works today (Slice 2)

- Every eligible application is assessed against the Role Packet: must-have and competency findings with verbatim quotes that code verifies against the resume or application text (unverifiable quotes are discarded and counted), unknowns for the screen to settle, and a recommendation (screen, hold, review manually). Gaps, career changes, missing degrees, and AI-written text never make a candidate "weak" (eval suite `triage.edge_cases`). Assessment is information; the stage moves to "Ready for your review".
- Policy `application.invite_screen` (default "do it and tell me"): a "screen" recommendation starts the screening workflow and the owner is told; "ask me first" creates a decision card on Home; "only a person" leaves an "Invite to a screen" button. An overridden eligibility rule always goes to the owner.
- Screening by web chat (an email link, no JavaScript needed) and by text when the candidate consented, inside 8:00 to 21:00 local time. Disclosure and "reply HUMAN" in the first message, one question at a time in the packet's order, at most two follow-ups per question, pay answered only from the posted range, protected details kept out of recorded answers, progress kept in code so a reload or a channel switch resumes. HUMAN or "Talk to a person" hands the conversation to a recruiter with the full transcript and a 4-hour SLA; WITHDRAW withdraws; STOP opts out of texts and revokes consent. A model outage gives an honest holding message; two in a row hand off to a person.
- Reminder after 48 hours, expiry after 72 more with a note to the owner; a completed screen gets a scorecard (per-question findings, competencies, pay expectation against the range, availability, eligibility confirmations, recommendation) and, under `application.recommend_interview`, moves to "Recommended for interview" or becomes a decision card.
- Owners can invite to a screen, mark for interview, hold, decline with a reason, or ask a recruiter from every candidate; the People page shows the screening conversation, both scorecards, and the assistant's pending suggestions. Home shows "What the assistant did".
- Stuck-role detector (daily per company): candidates waiting on the owner 3+ days, screen invites unanswered 3+ days, decisions pending 2+ days, no applicants in 14 days. Ladder: brief line → email with a one-tap link → offer of a recruiter (`intervention.offer_recruiter`) → recruiter escalation, with the `intervention.nudge` cooldown (48h) between rungs; interventions resolve when the owner acts.
- Plan limits: `text_screens` is checked before every invite and metered; exhaustion tells the owner and never pages a recruiter.
- Insufficient evidence is a state the owner can see (30 September 2026): an assessment that could not say enough (review manually, or "unknown") keeps the candidate in "Waiting on you" as "Needs a closer look", with the open questions in plain words on the Talent card, a filter for those rows, and a notification that names what is missing; "Ask about this in a screen" invites the candidate to a web screen that starts with exactly those questions; assessments say why the confidence is what it is; a résumé file with no text and a missing assessment are both said on the card, not only in the timeline (`apps/api/src/intelligence.test.ts`).
